billing questions.

something else? write to hello@dusk.bot.

is the free plan really free?{{i plus}}

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer or your own server with every feature. no card, no account and no trial timer.

what counts as work?{{i plus}}

using Metal at or for a business. that needs a separate commercial license from the upstream copyright holder. study, hobby projects and personal use are free. the license has the exact words.

how does yearly billing work?{{i plus}}

you pay for ten months and get twelve. plus is $200 a year, pro is $500 a year and team is $1,190 a year.

who bills me for the ai?{{i plus}}

your ai provider, on the plan or key you already have. a dusk plan pays for the cloud computer and the license, not for ai usage.

do i need an account?{{i plus}}

not for the free plan. an account at dusk.bot/dashboard is only needed for a paid plan, and it is also where you see your workspaces side by side.

how do i pay, and where are my invoices?{{i plus}}

by card at checkout. payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, Dusk Bot's merchant of record. your invoices show up on the billing page of your account. Dusk Bot does not store your card number.

can i cancel?{{i plus}}

any time, from the billing page of your account. cancelling a dusk plan does not cancel your ai subscriptions. those are separate and stay yours.

what happens to my bots if i stop paying?{{i plus}}

bots on your own computer or server are not affected. a cloud computer is kept for 30 days after the plan ends and is then deleted (we email you a week before), so move or save what you need first.
