the logo.

kit, a white pixel cat, on a sunrise sky over grass, in a rounded square. it sits to the left of the name. where the square is too much, kit alone does the job: white with its two dark eyes, on near black.

the bots.

twenty pixel bots, kit first. one solid colour each and two tall white eyes, nothing else: every mood is the body and the eyes. they are drawn in code, so they move. poke one.

a few words.

if you need a short description, this one is right.

Meet Metal, a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux where you run a team of AI bots and message them like teammates. Each bot has a job, a memory and a computer of its own, and runs on the AI account you already have. It is free for personal use.

do and don't

do

  • {{i check}}write the name as Metal, with a capital M.
  • {{i check}}leave the logo some room, on white or near black, and keep its rounded square.
  • {{i check}}show the bots as they are drawn: square pixels, one solid colour, two tall eyes.
  • {{i check}}let kit stand in for the logo where a square is too much.
  • {{i check}}use real pictures of the app when you show the app.

don't

  • {{i x}}redraw, recolour, stretch or tilt the logo, or put a new scene behind kit.
  • {{i x}}smooth a bot's pixels, or give it texture, outlines, shading, arms or a mouth.
  • {{i x}}give a bot two colours, or a colour that is not in its palette.
  • {{i x}}put the logo on a busy picture.
  • {{i x}}suggest we made, sponsor or endorse something we did not.
