Subprocessors

Effective date: 12 November 2026. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

These are the service providers dusk bot uses to run its services, what each one does and what data it handles. Our privacy policy explains the data in more detail. A provider's company location does not establish where all of its processing occurs.

dusk bot is local-first. Your bots, chats, files, memory and settings stay on your own computer or server, unless you choose a dusk cloud desktop. None of the providers below receive your messages, prompts or files, except InstaBox, which hosts your dusk cloud desktop if you have one, and Composio, which carries the actions you ask connected apps to take.

Not used

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks or a third-party error tracking service. Product analytics and error reports stay in our own database.

Changes

When we add or replace a sub-processor, we update this page and its date. If you would like to be told about changes, email privacy@dusk.bot. For questions about data processing agreements or transfer safeguards, also email privacy@dusk.bot.
