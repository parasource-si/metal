Metal/routines

a sunday evening preview of next week's calendar

paste one message and every sunday at 6pm a bot reads next week's calendar and tells you what's going to hurt: overlapping meetings, days with no lunch, the pitch you haven't prepped for.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

it suggests what to move and changes nothing. you walk into monday knowing, instead of finding out at 11:02.

the message

paste this into a chat with your bot

every sunday at 6pm, read my calendar for monday to friday next week.

for each day give me one or two lines: how many meetings, the first and last, and anything unusual. then flag, in this order:
- meetings that overlap
- days with no 30-minute gap between 11:30 and 2
- meetings with "review", "pitch" or "interview" in the title, so i can prepare

suggest what i could move, but don't change, accept or decline anything. if the week looks fine, just say "next week looks fine" and nothing else.
when
sundays at 18:00 in your time zone
needs
google calendar or outlook connected; reading is enough

what comes back

an example, not a real run

next week

mon: 3 meetings, 10:00 to 15:30. fine.
tue: 7 meetings, 9:00 to 18:00. no lunch gap.
wed: 2 meetings. quiet.
thu: 4 meetings. "pitch review" at 14:00, prepare the deck.
fri: 1 meeting.

clash: tue 11:00 "design sync" overlaps "1:1 with sam" (11:30).
suggestion: move the 1:1 to wednesday, which is open.

make it yours

  • change the window: "monday to sunday" or "just tomorrow" turns it into a daily look ahead
  • change what counts as prep: add the words that matter in your work, like "board", "demo" or "client"
  • send it somewhere else: "post it in my planning group chat", or "email it to me" (an email is a send, so it waits for Allow unless you set a daily amount; a bot on Full access with no sending rule may send up to 25 a day without asking, so set Ask every time for it)4
  • let it act: "if two meetings clash, ask me which to move and then move it" (this needs write access to the calendar)
  • add the inbox: "also list emails from this week that mention a meeting next week" if mail is connected too

when it goes wrong

  • it reads the wrong calendar. if you have several, name the ones you want: "only my work calendar and the team calendar". listing calendars is one of the things it can do, so ask it which ones it sees.
  • times are off by an hour or more. ask the bot "what time zone is this routine in?" and tell it yours; put your time zone in About me so every bot knows.
  • it didn't run. on your own computer nothing runs while it's asleep or Metal is closed; a run missed by less than 12 hours runs once when you're back. Automations in the sidebar shows what happened to each run.
  • the list is too long to read. say "shorter" or "only tell me about problems" after the first run; it keeps the change.

why sunday, and why it only reads

sunday night is the last cheap moment to fix a week. people can still say yes to a new time.

read only is the point. the bot needs only read access to your calendar, and Metal blocks any change from a bot that may only read. so you can switch it on for any bot without a second thought.

want it to move meetings later? give it write access and put "ask me before you move anything" in the same message. read its first few proposals before you say yes.

put your time zone in About me once. every routine then gets the hours right.

mistakes people make

pointing it at every calendar you own

name the two that matter: work and the team calendar.

no quiet rule

"if the week looks fine, just say so" keeps sunday short.

questions

does it work with outlook calendars?

yes. connect outlook instead of google calendar and send the same message.

can i pause it on holiday?

yes. the routine's card has Pause, and Automations in the sidebar lists every routine. pause it before you go and resume it when you're back.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
