an ai agent that controls a computer for you

hand over the clicking and keep the mouse within reach. a Metal bot can work through its own browser and desktop on cloud or your server.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

open screen to watch, take control for a step that needs you, then hand it back. on a mac, you can choose your own screen too. try a public-page lookup first.

what the four approval levels mean

start with ask for approval for commands and file changes. the levels below cover those actions. app sends use a separate setting; other app changes do not automatically ask first.

levelwhat it permits
Ask for approvalasks before risky steps1
Auto-accept editschanges files; asks for other steps1
Approve for methe ai reviews routine actions; you decide the rest1
Full accessallows everything; questions and missing sign-ins still need you1

give it a task you can recognise

get the contact instructions without hunting through the site yourself. use the desktop for looking, clicking and typing. try a public page or a document you chose. some sites will fight it. captchas and sign-ins are your job.

try: "open this public page and find its contact instructions. write them here. do not submit forms or create accounts. say if you cannot find them."

compare the answer with the page. screen shows the route it took.

choose the computer before the access

give it a separate desktop, or choose your mac screen on purpose. dusk runs on macos 12 or later, windows 10 or 11 in 64-bit, and ubuntu 24.04 in beta. only mac offers own-screen control.1

for a separate desktop:

  • dusk cloud: a desktop for each bot. plus keeps a good amount awake; pro is for heavy workloads
  • your own server: a private desktop there

bots, files and memory live there too. work after the lid closes needs an awake host. compare the places bots can live.

watch the first run

watch the first answer come together. choose new bot and give it one job. sign in to the ai you already use. dusk has no model of its own; your provider's terms and bill still apply.

start on ask for approval. send the public-page request and open screen. replies stream in; a line shows each tool in use.13

wrong page? send a correction. cmd . or ctrl . stops the bot. read the answer beside the page before giving it more work.

take control for sign-ins

take the mouse for sign-ins, then let the bot carry on. choose take control for passwords, captchas and two-step codes. click and type yourself, then hand control back. the bot stays signed in and never sees the password.1

even full access needs you for missing sign-ins. try the first task on a public page. add an account when the job needs it, and return control on the intended site.

give each app only the access it needs

read-only app access is enough for a report. connected apps give a bot another way to work. sign in under connected apps, choose the bots, then allow read only or an exact action list.

labels, meeting changes and page edits skip sending approval. for supervised edits, write "ask me before you change anything".

read the safety checks when the next job needs wider access.

graduate to a file you can replace

a proposed filing plan is a better next step than your whole home folder. attach a copy or drop it in chat. bot output appears there too. locally it lives in the bot's folder or your chosen project folder. cloud copies come down to documents/dusk bot.4

try file organisation as a proposal first. decide what should move after reading it.

schedule a job only once its input and finish are clear. local routines stop during sleep or while dusk is closed. seeing the screen isn't understanding it. look at the answer before you use it.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. make a bot for one public-page lookup
  2. leave it on ask for approval and give it the page link
  3. open screen and watch it find the contact instructions
  4. compare the answer with the page yourself

the useful part is the answer. watch it work, then read what it found.

mistakes people make

starting with your whole computer

use one public page, then a copy of one file.

treating full access as a fix for missing sign-ins

take control and finish the sign-in yourself.

assuming sending approval covers every app change

use read only for reviews, or put an ask-first instruction in the job.

quick checklist

  • the bot has the intended desktop
  • i can open screen and take control
  • the task has a clear stopping point
  • app access fits the task
  • connected apps allow only what this job needs

questions

can an ai agent control my windows pc?

use a separate bot desktop with the windows app. dusk cloud or your server provides it. the app supports windows 10 and 11, 64-bit; own-screen control is mac-only.

can i watch the bot and click something myself?

yes. screen shows it live. take control gives you the mouse and keyboard; hand control back lets the bot continue.

will it get through a captcha on its own?

plan to handle that yourself. take control for captchas, sign-ins and two-step codes. a higher approval level cannot remove a required human step.

can it keep working when i close the laptop?

only if the bot lives elsewhere. a sleeping laptop cannot keep its bot working. move the job to cloud or another awake host. watch and steer from your app.
