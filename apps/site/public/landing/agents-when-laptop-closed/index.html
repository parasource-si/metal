where do ai agents run when your laptop is closed?

nowhere. close the lid and an agent on your laptop stops: the 7am brief doesn't come, your phone can't reach it.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

to keep it working, it has to live on a computer that stays on. you have three choices: a spare machine at home, a server you rent and babysit, or a hosted computer like dusk cloud that someone else keeps running.

the four options side by side

Metal's own prices only. a spare machine and a rented server cost whatever you or your host charge. every option still uses your own ai plan.

why your laptop can't be the home

an agent is just a program. programs stop when the computer sleeps.

in Metal you feel it two ways:

  • routines don't run while the laptop sleeps or the app is closed. missed by less than 12 hours? it runs once when you're back. longer than that, it's skipped.
  • your phone can't reach the bots until the laptop wakes up.

Settings, Remote access, Keep this computer awake helps at your desk. it can't help in a backpack.

a spare computer at home

cheap, and it works, until it doesn't. an old laptop that never sleeps can host your bots for the price of its power.

the catch is you're the ops team. a power cut, a flaky router, an update that reboots it at 3am. if it falls over while you're travelling, nothing runs until someone at home pokes it.

a server you rent

great if you like servers. a lost weekend if you don't. your own server is free in Metal. you pay the host.

  • ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, 2 gb of memory or more
  • paste one line, wait about ten minutes
  • no ports, no domain; the app and server talk through a sealed relay

updates, backups and the bill are yours. use a fresh server, never one holding things you wouldn't hand a bot.

dusk cloud

a computer we host and look after. your bots just keep going. it has its own storage no one else can reach, backed up every day.

moving takes a minute: pick a plan, sign in under settings, dusk cloud, and the welcome brings your bots over with their chats, memory and routines. your ai sign-in stays yours. ai usage never lands on the dusk bill.

one honest warning: a bot working around the clock uses your ai plan around the clock. for heavy use, a bigger plan or an api key works out better.

which one fits you

pick by how much you'll miss the work when the lid's shut.

  • bots only while you're at your desk? stay on the laptop. it's free.
  • want the brief before you wake, or replies from your phone at night? that's the moment to move. most people pick dusk cloud because there's nothing to babysit.
  • you already run servers and enjoy it? your own server costs you only the server.
  • spare computer that never leaves the house? fine, as long as it stays on.

you can keep more than one and switch from the top of the sidebar. start on the laptop. move the day a missed routine annoys you.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. open Settings, Remote access and turn on Keep this computer awake for tonight
  2. set one routine for 7am
  3. check in the morning whether it ran (Automations shows each run)
  4. if it didn't, that's your sign to move it off the laptop

you don't have to choose forever. keep your laptop and add a cloud; switch from the sidebar.

mistakes people make

trusting a laptop with a 7am routine

a closed lid means no brief. move routines that must run on time.

a cheap server you never update

budget an hour a month for it, or don't rent one.

forgetting the ai plan runs all night too

watch your usage the first week on an always-on computer.

questions

can an ai agent run while my mac is asleep?

not on that mac. a sleeping computer runs nothing. keep it awake while plugged in, or move the agent to a computer that stays on.

do i need to know linux to keep an agent running 24/7?

no. dusk cloud needs no server skills. running your own server is the option that needs some.

what happens to my bots if i stop paying for dusk cloud?

bots on your own computer carry on. the cloud computer is kept for 30 days after the plan ends, then deleted, and we email you a week before. move your bots back or export a backup first.

is it cheaper to run it on my own server?

Metal charges nothing for it, so you pay only your hosting company. it's cheaper if your time looking after a server costs you nothing. the always-on cost calculator puts the numbers side by side.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
