using the ai you already sign in to for automation

put the ai you already pay for to work on a job you keep repeating. dusk runs vendors’ official command-line apps, with your own sign-in, an api key or a local model.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

your provider’s rules decide which route fits. as of 12 november 2026, those conditions differ. read the current terms for your setup before scheduling the work.

what the vendor pages say about sign-in

choose the provider route that permits your job. these vendor statements are as of 12 november 2026. read the conditions for the exact app and access method you intend to use.

what runs when you send a message

dusk brings the bot’s job and your chosen ai together. it has no model of its own. each bot keeps its chats and memory while using the ai you choose.

it runs official command-line apps such as claude code and codex cli on the machine where your bots live. sign in yourself in the app.8

your message goes from that machine straight to your ai provider. it does not pass through dusk. your provider bills usage on your plan.15

do not paste copied login secrets into an unknown app because a tutorial says it works. running a vendor’s program and handling its login yourself are different arrangements.

dusk is independent. anthropic, openai, google and xai do not own, fund or affiliate with it.14

anthropic distinguishes its own program from borrowed logins

you can use your own claude code sign-in under anthropic’s conditions. as of 12 november 2026, anthropic allows an end user’s own subscription sign-in to unmodified claude code, including on a hosted platform.1

that wording appeared between 16 and 30 august 2026.16 it comes with conditions:

  • run claude code as anthropic publishes it
  • do not remove or restrict its built-in sign-in methods
  • agree to commercial terms when hosting it
  • each person signs in with their own credentials
  • the platform cannot pay for, resell or intermediate their usage

the same legal page bars developers from offering claude.ai login in their own apps. they also cannot collect, store or handle those login secrets, or route requests through users’ plan credentials.1

a support article updated 7 october 2026 says signed-in agent sdk and claude -p still use plan limits.9 separate login guidance lets anthropic charge certain other tools to usage credits.10

read all three together before putting the job on a schedule.

google and xai have different boundaries

protect your google account before wrapping its cli in another app. as of 12 november 2026, antigravity’s terms call third-party software access a breach. google says it can suspend or terminate affected accounts.11

its faq also says wrapping agy inside other coding agents or apps is unsupported under consumer plans.5 take that seriously when choosing gemini access. an api key or local model offers another route.

xai’s grok build docs describe scripts, bots and acp use in other apps.6 its consumer terms forbid sharing account credentials.7 that does not settle every desktop app’s use of a sign-in.

since 12 october 2026, new customers get supergrok through the x subscription. existing standalone subscribers can keep their plan.12

the provider sign-in log dates the changes, so you can place an old forum answer.

choose an api key or local model when it fits

you do not have to make every job depend on a subscription login. dusk accepts api keys under settings, api keys. it supports anthropic, xai, openrouter, groq and services using the openai api.8

for local ai, run ollama or lm studio and choose use a local model. an api key still has a provider bill and terms. local setup has its own practical work too.

choose around the task and the access your provider allows. claude plans versus api walks through costs. scheduled ai tasks helps decide where repeating jobs should live.

questions

can i sign in to chatgpt from openclaw or codex?

openai supports chatgpt sign-in in codex cli as of 12 november 2026. it also lists openclaw, opencode, pi and t3 as apps using your plan. dusk is not on that list; it runs official codex cli. requests still use existing plan limits.23

what happens if my ai plan runs out?

the bot tells you. wait for the reset or switch ai. chats, files and memory stay. a dusk cloud payment adds no ai usage.81315

does a working sign-in mean the provider allows my setup?

no. it proves the login works. read current provider terms for the app and access method. the rules described here were checked on 12 november 2026.
