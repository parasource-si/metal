a gmail assistant that sorts, drafts and briefs you

connect gmail once and a bot reads your mail, files it and writes replies in your voice. it drafts on its own. it never sends unless you tap allow.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

you pick the ai behind it: claude, chatgpt, gemini, whatever you already use. a routine can hand you the three emails that matter before your coffee's done.

what a bot can do in Gmail

it works your inbox the way you would. search, open the thread, label it, write the reply, leave it in drafts. these are the actions that do the real work.

actionMetal treats it aswhat you would use it for
Fetch emailsreadsearch with gmail's own words, like from:acme newer_than:7d or is:unread
Fetch Message by Thread IDreadread a whole conversation before answering it, not just the last reply
Get Gmail attachmentreadopen the invoice or contract a message carries
Modify email labelssend: asks firstfile mail under labels like needs me or receipts
Create email draftchangewrite a reply and leave it in drafts for you to read
Update draftchangefix a draft when you say "shorter" or "warmer"
Send Emailsend: asks firstsend, only after you tap Allow (or within a daily amount you set)
Move to Trashchangeclear out what you told it to clear; tell it to check with you first

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 63 Gmail actions (version 20261105_00, read 12 november 20265). Metal treats 25 as reads, 8 as sends and 30 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like modify email labels, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Gmail

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Gmail and sign in on Gmail's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Gmail.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

more than one inbox? fine. connect each gmail account with a label like work or personal, then tell the bot which one you mean. up to five accounts per app.

on dusk cloud the connection is included. on your own computer it goes through Metal's own connection service, so there's no key to set up.

routines to copy

send one of these and it runs from then on. change the times and the words. that's the whole setup.

morning inbox brief

every weekday at 8, read what came into my gmail since yesterday evening. tell me the 3 to 5 things that need me today, one line each, with who it's from. skip newsletters and receipts. don't reply to anything.

weekdays at 8:00 · a short list in your chat, nothing changed in gmail

draft replies to clients

whenever an email arrives from anyone at @acme.com, read the whole thread and draft a reply in my voice: short, friendly, no exclamation marks. leave it in drafts and tell me it's there. never send it yourself.

when a matching email arrives (checked about every 15 minutes) · a draft in gmail and a one-line note in your chat

friday receipts sweep

every friday at 4, find this week's receipts and invoices in gmail, add the label receipts to each, and give me a table of sender, amount and date. if an amount is over $500, put it at the top.

fridays at 16:00 · labelled emails and a table in your chat

what it asks you first

reading and drafting just happen. sending waits for you. anything that leaves your outbox (send, reply, forward) stops at a card with Allow and Deny, in the app, on your phone or in a call.

  • want it to send a few on its own? set a daily amount, per bot.
  • careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.
  • want it hands-off entirely? allow gmail for that bot as read only. Metal blocks every change before it reaches google.

your google password never reaches a bot. you sign in on google's own page, composio holds the connection, and Disconnect removes it there too.

what it cannot do

  • set filters, your vacation reply, imap, pop, forwarding or send-as. the standard connection doesn't get those google permissions. the bot tells you instead of retrying. your own composio project can add them.
  • work while your laptop sleeps. on your own computer, routines pause when it's asleep or Metal is closed. a run missed by less than 12 hours runs once when you're back. dusk cloud doesn't sleep.
  • jump the second mail lands. gmail is checked about every 15 minutes, so "when an email arrives" can take that long.
  • read more than five gmail accounts, or one you connected somewhere else. connect each one from Metal.
  • unsend. once you tap allow, it's gone, same as when you send it yourself.

which ai does the work

any of them. you choose per bot. Metal has no model of its own. sign in to claude, chatgpt, gemini or grok, use an api key, or run a model on your own machine.

so "claude for gmail" and "chatgpt for gmail" are the same setup here. connect gmail once, pick the ai in the bot's model picker, swap any time. chats and memory stay put.

what you say to the bot goes from your machine to that ai, on your plan with them. not through us.

teach it how you write

tell it once. it remembers. "i sign off with just my first name." "dana approves anything over $500." "never reply to recruiters."

those become notes you can read and edit under the bot's Memory. a week of small fixes ("shorter", "skip newsletters") and most people stop editing the drafts at all.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. open Connected apps and sign in to gmail on google's page
  2. make a bot and give it one job: your inbox
  3. send it the morning brief routine below
  4. read tomorrow's brief and tell it what to cut

start read only for a day. if the brief is right, then let it label and draft.

mistakes people make

giving the inbox bot every app

it only needs gmail. keep your files and slack out of its reach.

letting it send in week one

keep sending on Ask every time until the drafts sound like you.

a routine with no quiet rule

add "if nothing needs me, say so in one line" or it'll pad the brief.

questions

can it work with two gmail accounts?

yes. connect each one with its own label, like work and personal, and say which you mean: "brief me on work mail only". up to five accounts per app.

will it send email without asking me?

not unless you let it. sending asks first by default. you can set a daily amount a bot may send on its own, per bot, and change it back at any time.

does Metal see my email?

the bot reads it to do the job, on the machine your bots run on, and the text goes to the ai you picked. Metal's account never sees your messages. see privacy and your data.

what does it cost?

the app is free for personal use on your own computer. the ai is billed by your ai provider on your plan with them. if you want routines to run while your laptop is shut, dusk cloud starts at $20 a month.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
