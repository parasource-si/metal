a stripe bot that reads billing before money moves

find the overdue invoice before the billing question becomes an awkward call. dusk brings stripe invoices, subscriptions and refund records into chat.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

ask for amounts, currencies and dates with read-only access. you get the evidence without giving the bot a way to move money. add refund access later only with a sending rule and a review of each amount.

what a bot can do in Stripe

get the billing answer without moving a cent. the first seven actions read records. create refund is the action to add only when you want money returned.

actionMetal treats it aswhat you would use it for
List Invoicesreadreview invoices in the period you are closing
Search invoicesreadfind invoices for a particular billing question
Retrieve customerreadconfirm which customer a record belongs to
List subscriptionsreadinspect subscription records before explaining billing status
List Refundsreadmatch this week's refunds to the charges they came from
Retrieve Charge Detailsreadread the charge behind a refund request
List Payoutsreadreview payout records separately from invoice totals
Create Refundsend: asks firstmake a specific refund only after you choose Allow

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 435 Stripe actions (version 20261101_00, read 12 november 20264). Metal treats 206 as reads, 64 as sends and 165 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like add lines to invoice, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Stripe

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Stripe and sign in on Stripe's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Stripe.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

start with a billing reader. connect stripe under connected apps and give the bot read-only access. stripe handles sign-in.

for refunds later, allow only the required reads and create refund. use ask every time with no daily allowance.2

routines to copy

keep money movement out of scheduled reports. these jobs read different billing records and bring back evidence. you decide what needs action.

overdue invoice review

every monday at 8, read stripe invoices still open after their due date. list customer, invoice reference, amount, currency and due date here. group by currency. send no invoices, contact nobody and change no records. if there are none, say so briefly.

mondays at 08:00 · an overdue invoice list, grouped by currency, for your review

refund reconciliation

every friday at 3, read stripe refunds created this week and their related charges. show each refund reference, amount, currency and charge reference here. flag anything you cannot match. don't create another refund or move money.

fridays at 15:00 · a refund-to-charge table with unmatched records called out

subscription questions

on the first weekday of each month at 10, read the customer list in your memory notes. inspect their stripe subscription records and report status and billing dates here. flag missing context and stop if the list is missing. don't cancel, update or charge anything.

first weekday of the month at 10:00 · a customer billing brief with dates and unanswered questions

what it asks you first

keep the refund decision beside the customer, charge, currency and amount. refunds, charges, transfers and invoice payments count as sends. with ask every time, they wait for allow.

these actions are ordinary writes and can act without that prompt:

  • confirm payment intent
  • capture payment intent
  • create credit note
  • create subscription
  • cancel subscription
  • finalize an invoice
  • void an invoice

keep billing readers read only. a separate refund bot needs only create refund and the required reads.

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.2

what it cannot do

  • refund or edit billing with read-only access. dusk blocks those calls before they reach stripe.
  • complete your accounts from invoice totals. group currencies separately and follow up unexplained differences.
  • run a local report while the computer sleeps. use dusk cloud or another awake machine when it must arrive early.

keep collection separate from review

bring the overdue list to the person who knows the customer. the bot can find references and amounts. you may know why a payment is late.

the invoice follow-up routine covers drafting the next message. decide who needs a reminder after reading the records.

choose where billing summaries land

start with a report here. if you later want a google sheets summary, connect it separately and name the destination range. reporting doesn't require subscription edits.

for a founder brief, ask which billing questions need your decision. don't turn incomplete records into a revenue forecast.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. connect stripe with read-only access
  2. ask for one overdue invoice and its currency
  3. read its customer, amount and due date beside the invoice
  4. keep the first report here without customer contact

your billing reader does not need permission to refund. leave that action out until you have a separate reason for it.

quick checklist

  • the billing bot has only read access
  • amounts stay beside their currencies
  • each refund points to its charge
  • unmatched records stay visible for review

mistakes people make

assuming all billing changes wait for allow

some stripe actions are writes. read-only access is the clearer boundary for a reporting bot.

adding invoice totals across currencies

group currencies separately. keep each amount tied to its source record.

turning an overdue list into automatic reminders

review context first. reporting and contacting customers are different jobs.

questions

can a stripe ai agent issue refunds automatically?

read-only access blocks refunds. for a refund bot, choose ask every time and review each amount before allow. use the sending rule above for full-access bots too.2

can it check billing without access to move money?

yes, keep stripe read only. ask for invoice, subscription or refund reports here. that permission blocks money-changing actions.

is this financial advice or a record review?

it is a record review. the routines identify questions. they don't decide tax treatment, complete your accounts or tell you who deserves a refund.
