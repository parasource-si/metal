sort a google sheets range without splitting records

put dispatch in due-date order, with every customer still beside their own job. the bot can sort a google sheets range by several columns.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

later rules break ties, such as customer name after due date. include the full width of each record, with headers and totals outside. ask it to show the range and wait before reordering.

what it does

a date must travel with its customer and quantity. sorting one column can separate values that belong together. give the bot the sheet link, tab and full record width.

keep headers and totals outside the range. name the last row or ask the bot to find where records end.

put rules in order: due date first, customer second. the second rule only breaks ties in the first.

when you would use it

  • put dispatch in order. sort by due date, then customer for matching dates.
  • find attendees faster. alphabetise complete records while leaving the header above.
  • group the job list. sort by stage, then scheduled date within each stage.

try it

paste this into a chat with your bot

every thursday at 16:30, inspect the dispatch tab in the ottervale joinery spreadsheet. find all current job records and include every record column. exclude the header and totals. propose sorting by due date ascending, then customer ascending. show the exact range and both rules here. ask me before you change anything and wait for agreement. sort only that range, then confirm completion here. never sort one record column on its own.
when
thursdays at 16:30
needs
google sheets read and write for inspecting and sorting the agreed range

what comes back looks like this (an example)

ottervale dispatch sort proposal
records: dispatch a2:f24
first: due date, earliest first
then: customer, a to z
header and totals excluded. waiting before reordering.

set it up

  1. limit the access. connect sheets with read and write for inspection and sorting.
  2. describe the table. include its link, tab, full record width and where records end.
  3. choose rule priority. name directions and tie-breakers. require a range preview and your agreement.
  4. follow a familiar customer. their date and quantity should travel with them. pause the routine if the layout changes.

how Metal handles it

Metal counts Sort Range as a change: it alters something in Google Sheets, and the sending setting does not cover it. a bot you allow only to read can never call it.

the permissions Google Sheets asks for to run it: spreadsheets, drive.file, drive.

where it stops

  • everything in the range moves. include full rows of record data and leave headers and totals out.
  • rule order changes the answer. customer first gives a different queue from due date first.
  • follow the data after sorting. open a familiar customer’s row and confirm its date still belongs to it.

try it in 2 minutes

  2. ask for the full record range without headers or totals
  3. name the first sort rule and tie-breaker
  4. review that plan before sorting

quick checklist

  • full record width included
  • header and totals excluded
  • sort directions and priority clear
  • review required before reordering

mistakes people make

sorting only the date column

include the customer, quantity and every other record column.

letting the header join the sort

preview the first and last rows before allowing the change.

questions

can it sort by two columns?

yes. put the rules in priority order. the second breaks ties where the first column has matching values.

will sorting wait for me to approve it?

only if you tell the bot to wait. the sending setting does not pause this edit. include an ask-before-changing instruction.
