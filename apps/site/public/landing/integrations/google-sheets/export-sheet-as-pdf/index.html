Metal/integrations/google sheets

make a pdf of the google sheets tab you chose

get a printable roster without exporting the budget beside it. a bot can export one google sheets tab or rectangle as a pdf.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

give the original sheet link and tab name, then choose portrait, gridlines or fit to width. dusk counts export as a change, so it needs read and write access. your source cells stay untouched.

what it does

get the right rows onto readable pages. paste the normal sheet link and name the tab or rectangle. the export works on that selection rather than guessing which workbook content you want.

choose orientation, margins, sheet names and repeated frozen rows. google leaves some print settings, including margins, unclear. fit to width can still leave tiny text. open the pdf at normal size.

when you would use it

  • print the duty roster alone. keep budget tabs out of the export.
  • show only the quote that matters. choose its approved rectangle for review.
  • give a wide attendance table room. try landscape.
  • file a clean totals table. turn gridlines off for the pdf.

try it

paste this into a chat with your bot

every tuesday at 15:45, open the fernwater production spreadsheet saved in the production folder. confirm the rehearsal roster tab and export its current version as a landscape pdf. fit it to width and repeat frozen rows. ask me before exporting. put the pdf here for me to read. never include other tabs, change cells or email the result.
when
tuesdays at 15:45
needs
google sheets connected with read and write for pdf export; access to the production folder containing the spreadsheet link

what comes back looks like this (an example)

fernwater rehearsal roster
tab: rehearsal roster
layout: landscape, fit to width, frozen rows repeated
pdf attached. open it at normal size to read the column headings.

set it up

  1. connect google sheets with read and write. allow the pdf export action.
  2. paste the original sheet link with the chosen tab open. published viewing links won't work.
  3. name the tab or whole cell block and choose orientation. ask for review before export if wanted.
  4. open the pdf. look for clipped edges and awkward page breaks before keeping the schedule.

what it needs from you

inputrequiredwhat it is
sheet_idyesNumeric ID of the sheet tab to export (the gid in a Sheets URL), not the tab title or spreadsheet ID.
spreadsheet_idyesGoogle Sheets spreadsheet ID or original docs.google.com Sheets URL, not the spreadsheet title.
portraitnoWhether to use portrait orientation, mapped to portrait.
top_marginnoTop margin passed to the sample-backed top_margin control.
left_marginnoLeft margin passed to the sample-backed left_margin control.
fit_to_widthnoValue for the sample-backed fitw PDF print control.

from composio's catalog for Google Sheets (action GOOGLESHEETS_EXPORT_SHEET_AS_PDF, read 12 november 20264). your bot fills these in from what you tell it.

how Metal handles it

Metal counts Export Sheet as PDF as a change: it alters something in Google Sheets, and the sending setting does not cover it. a bot you allow only to read can never call it.

the permissions Google Sheets asks for to run it: drive, drive.readonly, spreadsheets, spreadsheets.readonly.

where it stops

  • copy the link from the right tab. it tells the bot which one to export.
  • published viewing links won't work. use the original spreadsheet link, not a /d/e/ address.
  • give the whole rectangle. say a1:f30 rather than asking the bot to guess the edge.
  • look at the margins in the pdf. google leaves some print settings unclear, so the file itself is your guide.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. open the tab you want and copy its normal link
  2. ask for that tab as a landscape pdf
  3. choose gridlines and repeated frozen rows
  4. open the file at normal size and read the smallest text

repeat frozen rows when the reader needs column headings on every printed page.

before you share the pdf

  • only the intended tab or rectangle appears
  • the smallest text is readable
  • headings and page breaks make sense
  • no source cells changed

mistakes people make

copying a published viewing link

open the original spreadsheet and copy its link instead.

trusting fit to width

open the pdf at normal size. fitting everything can make it unreadable.

questions

can i export only part of a tab?

yes. give a rectangular range. name the whole block, such as a1:f30, so the bot doesn't guess where your table ends.

will asking every time for sends approve this export?

no. dusk treats export as a change. put 'ask me before exporting' in the routine if you want that checkpoint.
