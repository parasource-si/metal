Metal/integrations/google calendar

change a calendar detail without replacing the event

send visitors to the right entrance without rebuilding the meeting. the bot can fix a google calendar location, title or time while leaving other details alone.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

name the calendar and event date. guest lists and repeat rules replace their whole existing lists, so keep those outside a simple room correction. ask for a preview and a pause before the edit.

what it does

the appointment can stay while the address changes. have the bot find it, confirm the date and calendar, then change only the detail you name.

  • guest and repeat schedules replace the whole existing list
  • adding one guest means reading the current guest list first
  • update emails follow your account default unless you choose yes or no

say what should stay, and whether guests should hear about the edit.

when you would use it

  • save visitors a wrong turn. correct the room without touching guests.
  • make the title useful. replace a vague name with the agreed workshop title.
  • move the right appointment. confirm the date before changing its start time.
  • fix stale directions. replace the old entrance in the description.

try it

paste this into a chat with your bot

every monday at 12:30, read emails received in my work gmail during the last seven days from our studio bookings team. find explicit room corrections for studio visits this week. match each to an event on my work google calendar. show the event date, current location and proposed location here. ask me before you change anything. change only locations i approve and turn guest update emails off. leave attendees, repeat schedules, meeting links and times alone. ask about unclear matches.
when
mondays at 12:30
needs
gmail read only; google calendar read and write for finding and correcting events

what comes back looks like this (an example)

current: north entrance

only the location would change.

set it up

  1. connect the calendar. give the bot read and write, limited to the actions it needs.
  2. name the meeting precisely. include calendar, title and date. the bot finds the event.
  3. require a preview. ask for old and new values, then a wait for your agreement.
  4. open the changed event. confirm its location after the first edit and narrow unclear matching rules.

from composio's catalog for Google Calendar (action GOOGLECALENDAR_PATCH_EVENT, read 12 november 20264). your bot fills these in from what you tell it.

how Metal handles it

Metal counts Patch Event as a change: it alters something in Google Calendar, and the sending setting does not cover it. a bot you allow only to read can never call it.

the permissions Google Calendar asks for to run it: calendar, calendar.app.created, calendar.events, calendar.events.owned.

where it stops

  • repeating meetings need a date. say which occurrence you want changed.
  • a guest list replaces everyone. the bot must include current guests when adding someone.
  • guest emails follow the edit’s own choice. sending settings do not decide this. say yes or no in the request.
  • times need a zone. without one, the bot may interpret the time as utc.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. name the calendar and event date
  2. ask for its current location
  3. propose a new location with guest emails off
  4. review before allowing the edit

a room correction should not quietly become a guest-list edit.

quick checklist

  • correct calendar and occurrence
  • only intended details will change
  • guest update choice says yes or no
  • new time includes its time zone

mistakes people make

adding one guest as a list of one

read the current guests first or the edit can remove everyone else.

moving “the friday meeting”

give the exact date and time zone before anything changes.

questions

can it change just the address and keep the rest?

yes. ask for a location-only change and a preview of the meeting first. details left out of the edit stay as they are.

what happens if i ask it to add one attendee?

the new list replaces the old one. the bot needs to read everyone already invited and include them too.

will my guests get an email about the change?

say whether guests should hear about it. tell the bot to notify guests or keep update emails off. leaving it out uses your google default.
