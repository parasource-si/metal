a canva bot for finding assets and preparing exports

let the bot find the files before you open ten canva tabs. it can inspect designs, organise assets and start exports using your chosen ai.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

that helps with a handoff, not individual canvas edits. give it one folder or design and a clear result. you still decide whether the artwork is ready to leave.

what a bot can do in Canva

find the file that goes to the printer before opening ten tabs. get design titles, available formats and the assets behind the handoff. the actions below also cover uploads and asset updates within the limits you choose.

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 53 Canva actions (version 20261101_00, read 12 november 20264). Metal treats 27 as reads, 8 as sends and 18 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like create comment reply in design (deprecated), and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Canva

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Canva and sign in on Canva's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Canva.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

get the inventory right before handing over the file. connect canva in connected apps and sign in on canva’s page. give the design-operations bot a folder or design reference.

start with an inventory. once the titles match, allow the export or asset actions the handoff needs.

routines to copy

repeat the handoff without repeating the hunt. list the folder, resolve unclear versions and get the chosen exports ready. creative approval stays with you.

campaign design index

every tuesday at 2, list designs in my launch folder in canva. give their titles here and links only when canva returns them. keep fetching results until none remain. flag titles that leave the intended version unclear. never rename or move items.

tuesdays at 14:00 · design titles with unclear versions flagged

export readiness

each thursday at 11, inspect designs in my ready for handoff folder in canva. list their available export formats and propose files to export here. ask me before you start an export. use only post exports for starting jobs. wait for each agreed export result to say it finished before calling the file ready. do not post comments or publish anything.

thursdays at 11:00 · available formats, then finished export results after your agreement

asset naming proposals

on the first friday each month at 1, inspect assets in my campaign assets folder in canva. compare their names and tags with the naming guide in my project folder. propose renames here, showing current and proposed names. never update tags, move assets or delete anything.

first friday of the month at 13:00 · a rename proposal against your written naming guide

what it asks you first

one of canva's two export actions counts as a send and asks first. the other doesn't. limit the bot to post exports, or keep "ask me before you start an export" in the routine. a daily sending amount skips individual approvals. canva comments use sending controls too.3

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.3

asset renames, moves and upload jobs do not automatically ask. keep an inventory bot on inspection actions; tell a writer to ask before making those changes. preparing an export leaves commenting, sharing and publishing as separate jobs.

what it cannot do

  • edit any canvas element you point at. this action list covers designs, jobs, assets, folders and comments. it has no general action for rewriting individual elements.
  • promise every export format. read the design’s available formats before choosing a file.
  • start from a canva change event. canva has no events here. use a schedule and an awake computer, or dusk cloud.

follow the export through to the finished file

wait for the export result to say the file is ready. starting the job and getting its result take separate actions. ask for both. follow uploads through to completion too.

if the handoff belongs in google drive, connect drive separately and name the folder. the printer should get a file, not another search job.

keep creative approval beside the brief

bring the chosen design back to the brief. the bot can find files and suggest names. you still decide whether the artwork says the right thing.

figma handoffs use different actions. read that list before planning the same job there. start social posting after choosing what is ready.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. connect canva and choose a reading bot
  2. name one campaign folder
  3. ask for design titles and unclear versions
  4. open a result and confirm it is the design you meant

ask for design titles first. only promise links when canva returns them.

mistakes people make

calling an export ready when it starts

read the separate export result before promising the file.

using “final” as the only version rule

name a ready-for-handoff folder and keep uncertain versions out.

questions

can chatgpt or claude work with my canva designs?

yes, through the listed canva actions. choose the bot’s ai and connect canva. it cannot do everything available in the canvas editor.

can it automatically export a set of designs?

it can start exports and follow their results. name the designs and choose from their available formats. the send-type export asks by default. the other export action doesn't, so say "ask me first".3

do canva routines keep running when my laptop closes?

local routines stop when it sleeps or dusk closes. dusk cloud keeps the bot on a running machine while your computer is off.
