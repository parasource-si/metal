a slack assistant that finds the decision in the thread

get yesterday's decision without spending the morning in slack. a Metal bot searches channels, reads the replies and brings you the outcome with a source link.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

you choose the ai behind it. keep a catch-up private in dusk chat, or let it propose a reply for you to post. read-only slack access is enough for both.

what a bot can do in Slack

get the answer at the end of the thread. the opening message might suggest friday; ten replies later, everyone agreed thursday. give the bot a channel and question so it reads the conversation around each match.

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 171 Slack actions (version 20261101_00, read 12 november 20265). Metal treats 67 as reads, 8 as sends and 96 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like send message, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Slack

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Slack and sign in on Slack's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Slack.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

start with a channel and reading access.

connect slack from connected apps and select the bot that may read it. slack handles sign-in; the bot never sees your password.

name the channel and any threads to exclude before scheduling a catch-up.

routines to copy

pick the moment you keep losing time to. these routines cover catching up, spotting support questions and preparing handovers. send one to your bot and name the channel.

yesterday's decisions

every weekday at 11, read yesterday's #cresselwick-launch messages and their replies. list decisions, open questions and proposed dates here, with a message link for each. keep proposals separate from agreements. never post or change slack.

questions that still need a reply

when a new message arrives in #cresselwick-help, see whether it asks a product question. read the thread before deciding whether it still needs a reply. if it does, show the question and suggested wording here. never post or react.

when a new message arrives in the named help channel · a suggested reply in dusk chat, after reading the thread

friday handover

every friday at noon, read this week's #cresselwick-operations discussion and pinned instructions. draft a handover here with unfinished work and people named for it. mark missing owners unknown. never assign work or post the draft.

fridays at 12:00 · a handover draft with unfinished work and named owners

what it asks you first

a private catch-up only needs reading access. the bot can draft in dusk chat while posting, reactions and channel changes stay blocked.

want it to post? allow the send action and choose Ask every time for an Allow or Deny card. you can grant a daily amount instead.

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.

scheduling a message and editing a posted one count as writes, not sends. exclude them, or put "ask me before you change anything" in the routine. channel settings and other writes need that review rule too.

what it cannot do

  • find conversations the account cannot access. a workspace search does not reveal every company discussion.
  • post with reading access alone. it can propose text in dusk chat. posting needs the allowed action and follows sending rules.
  • work overnight on a sleeping desktop. slack events arrive at once, but the bot needs a running machine. dusk cloud keeps routines running.

keep the source beside the claim

a useful summary lets you open the discussion. ask for message links. keep decisions apart from ideas that nobody confirmed.

the channel digest routine gives you a recurring version. exclude sensitive channels unless the brief actually needs them.

follow the link to the work

keep the reference beside the decision. the work may live in github, or the agreed plan in notion. include those links in the summary.

if the bot needs to open that workspace, connect the app separately. slack access covers the discussion; the linked work has its own access.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. connect slack for reading
  2. name one project channel
  3. ask for yesterday's confirmed decisions with links
  4. open the confirming reply behind one decision

the last reply can matter more than the first message. ask the bot to read both.

mistakes people make

summarising only the parent message

read the replies. they may reverse the original proposal.

granting posting access for a private brief

keep it read only. drafts can stay in dusk chat.

questions

can a slack ai bot summarise a whole thread?

yes. the thread action reads replies. ask for those before summarising, especially when the opening message only proposes an idea.

will it talk in my channels on its own?

read-only access blocks posting. if you allow sends, choose Ask every time to read each post before it goes out.

can i read the catch-up from my phone?

yes, after pairing to the bots' home. a paired computer must stay awake with dusk open. dusk cloud works with your computer off.
