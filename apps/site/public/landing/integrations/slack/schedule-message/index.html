queue a slack message for later

write it on friday. let slack deliver it when the monday shift starts. a bot can queue a channel message, direct message or thread reply for a future time.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

give the destination, wording, time and time zone. once queued, slack posts it later without another yes, so review the note before scheduling.

schedule message, in plain words

your working day need not become someone else’s notification. slack accepts future messages up to 120 days ahead. give your local time zone; the bot handles the conversion. delivery must still be in the future when it queues.

the connected account must belong to the destination channel. for a thread, link the original message. layout attachments mean formatting here; document uploads need another action.

a routine to copy

paste this into a chat with your bot

every friday at 14:40, prepare this note for #larkvale-studio: "please put monday’s equipment requests in the booking list".

- show the text and channel here
- propose delivery next monday at 08:45 europe/london
- wait for my agreement before scheduling

after queueing, report the confirmed delivery time here. if the result is unclear, look for the queued note before trying again. never queue a second copy blindly.
when
fridays at 14:40; monday delivery follows your review
needs
slack connected with read and write; membership in #larkvale-studio

what comes back looks like this (an example)

larkvale studio reminder
channel: #larkvale-studio
delivery: monday, 08:45 europe/london
preview ready: the booking-list reminder will enter the queue after your agreement.

good jobs for it

  • respect a later shift. queue a reminder for when colleagues start.
  • prepare workshop instructions. schedule the reviewed text in the session thread.
  • time an office closure notice. write it during your working day for delivery later.

before it runs

Metal counts Schedule message as a change: it alters something in Slack, and the sending setting does not cover it. a bot you allow only to read can never call it.

the permissions Slack asks for to run it: chat:write.

how to start

  1. allow scheduling deliberately. connect slack with read and write for this bot.
  2. name the destination. give the channel and local time zone. link the parent message for a thread reply.
  3. require a pause. say "wait for my agreement before scheduling"; send approval does not cover queueing.
  4. open the queued note. confirm its delivery time before setting a weekly reminder.

limits worth knowing

  • past times fail. so do dates beyond 120 days ahead.
  • private channels still need membership. a name does not grant access.
  • link previews may still appear. avoid links whose preview could reveal private details.

review the note before queueing. slack will not ask you again when delivery time arrives.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. choose one channel you belong to
  2. write a short note and tomorrow’s local delivery time
  3. ask the bot to preview it without queueing
  4. read the text, channel and time zone before allowing it

before the note enters the queue

  • the destination is the right channel or thread
  • the text is ready for other people to read
  • the local time and time zone are explicit
  • the bot will look for a queued note after an unclear result before retrying

mistakes people make

trusting "ask every time" for scheduled notes

add a wait-for-agreement instruction. scheduling does not use send approval.

saying "9 tomorrow" across time zones

state the time zone and compare it with the confirmed delivery time.

questions

will send approval stop it from scheduling?

no, scheduling counts as a write. tell the bot to wait for your agreement before it queues anything.

can it attach a document to the scheduled note?

not through this action. it schedules message content and layout. uploading a file takes a separate action.
