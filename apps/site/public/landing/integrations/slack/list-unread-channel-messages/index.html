Metal/integrations/slack

catch up on unread slack posts without posting a reply

catch up privately before you jump into the channel. the bot reads unread slack posts and tells you what needs attention here.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

unread follows your connected account's reading position. read only is enough. this list leaves out thread replies, and starts at the oldest posts if slack has no saved reading position.

what it does

get back from lunch knowing what needs you. unread follows your slack account's reading position, rather than the bot's memory. name the channel and let the bot find it. your connected account must belong to that channel, private channel or group dm.

have the bot continue through to the newest post. thread replies need a separate read; someone may already have answered the question there.

when you would use it

  • catch unread duty-channel announcements after lunch
  • skim the group conversation before the evening shift
  • pull out questions from status posts without replying to anyone

try it

paste this into a chat with your bot

every weekday at 13:35, read unread main-channel posts in greywick duty through my connected slack account. finish all result batches. group questions, notices and other updates here. say clearly that you have not checked thread replies. if no posts are unread, say so in one line. never post, react or change my reading position.
when
weekdays at 13:35
needs
slack read only; your connected account belongs to the duty channel

what comes back looks like this (an example)

greywick duty: unread channel posts
questions: soren asked who has the spare gate key.
notices: east entrance closed for cleaning.
updates: evening rota shared.
thread replies still to read.

set it up

  1. connect the slack account whose unread position you want; allow read only
  2. name the channel and have the bot find it
  3. try one catch-up and open the threads behind questions that need you
  4. read the next run on the schedule card; pause lunchtime catch-ups during leave

from composio's catalog for Slack (action SLACK_LIST_UNREAD_CHANNEL_MESSAGES, read 12 november 20264). your bot fills these in from what you tell it.

how Metal handles it

Metal counts List unread channel messages as a read: it looks and changes nothing. a bot you allow only to read can use it.

the permissions Slack asks for to run it: channels:read, groups:read, im:read, mpim:read.

where it stops

  • channel posts leave out thread replies. fetch the relevant threads before treating a question as unanswered.
  • no saved reading position can bring old posts back. ask the bot to flag that gap.
  • the default batch holds 25 posts. the bot should continue until it finishes.
  • sleep stops local catch-ups. dusk cloud keeps the scheduled routine running.

the channel asks the question. the thread may already have the answer.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. connect slack with read only
  2. name one channel you belong to
  3. ask for unread posts grouped by what needs you
  4. read threads before acting on open questions

mistakes people make

calling a channel question unanswered

read its thread before chasing someone.

using the wrong connected account

connect the account whose unread dots you want

questions

does the summary include unread thread replies?

not with this action alone. ask for a separate thread read when a post needs more context.

why do i see posts i already remember reading?

slack starts at the oldest posts without a saved reading position. use the account whose unread dots you want and have the bot flag when that position is missing.
