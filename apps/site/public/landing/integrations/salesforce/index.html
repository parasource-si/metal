a salesforce assistant that checks the record first

find the deal with no next step on monday, before it slips at quarter end. a Metal bot can search salesforce opportunities, leads and follow-up tasks with read-only access.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

get the owner, close date and missing next step together in chat. creating tasks or changing deals needs write access. start with the review; let the owner answer the gap.

what a bot can do in Salesforce

choose the record type and review question first.

  • an opportunity review should cite opportunity details.
  • a lead intake check should stay with lead fields.
  • these actions support those jobs without making the bot roam through every crm object.
actionMetal treats it aswhat you would use it for
Search opportunitiesreadpull every deal closing this month before the monday meeting
Get opportunityreadopen a deal before describing its next step
Search leadsreadfind leads matching the intake criteria you set
Get leadreadinspect a lead before flagging missing information
Search tasksreadfind the follow-up that should happen before the deal closes
Search contactsreadlocate the contact relevant to a customer question
Create taskchangerecord an agreed follow-up as a new task
Update opportunitychangefix a field you asked it to fix, nothing else

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 327 Salesforce actions (version 20261104_00, read 12 november 20264). Metal treats 175 as reads, 10 as sends and 142 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like create campaign record, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

connect Salesforce

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Salesforce and sign in on Salesforce's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Salesforce.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

give the bot the fields that define your question.

allow salesforce for your sales bot in connected apps. start with the read actions needed for an opportunity and task review. name the team, records and fields.

if your crm uses terms in a special way, explain them before scheduling.

routines to copy

keep the pipeline where it is. bring the gaps here. define the fields your team uses. these routines read records and ask questions; they don't rewrite stages or owners.

close-date review

every monday at 8, find our team's salesforce opportunities expected to close this month. read the deal details and related follow-up tasks where available. list those with no saved next step here. show the saved close date and owner. leave both alone. flag any records you cannot read.

mondays at 8:00 · deals with missing next steps for the pipeline meeting

lead intake gaps

every weekday at 2, review new leads in salesforce that match our intake criteria. flag missing company or contact details. separate those gaps from leads ready for our qualification check. link findings to the records here. never guess field values, enrich details or convert leads.

weekdays at 14:00 · an intake checklist beside the source lead records

friday follow-up audit

every friday at 10, find my salesforce tasks due this week. compare their saved status with related opportunity details you can read. list unfinished follow-ups and unclear links here. leave task statuses alone. if all are complete, say so in one line.

fridays at 10:00 · unfinished follow-ups and unclear links in your chat

what it asks you first

keep the review read only until you want record changes. that blocks task creation and opportunity edits. if you later allow writes, put "ask me before you change anything" in the job. the sending setting does not hold record changes.

sends ask by default unless you set a daily amount. careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.3

what it cannot do

  • it cannot change stages, owners or tasks with read-only access. allow specific writes only if record maintenance becomes part of the job.
  • it cannot fill gaps without evidence. report an empty field as empty and ask its owner.
  • dusk classes query contacts by name as a write, so a read-only bot can't use it. use the search actions above instead.
  • a sleeping local computer cannot deliver the review. keep dusk open and the machine awake, or use dusk cloud.

define what counts as a next step

find the ownerless next step on monday, rather than at quarter end. tell the bot where your team saves next steps. a linked task and a vague note need different follow-up. ask it to bring both into the review.

use lead research for a separate company brief. if your pipeline lives in hubspot, start with its own record actions.

mail needs its own connection

bring the customer thread into the deal review when it matters. connect gmail separately and name the threads to read. salesforce access alone does not include that inbox.

keep the crm finding beside the email evidence. missing correspondence belongs in the questions for the owner.

an empty next-step field is a question for the owner.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. connect salesforce with only the needed read actions
  2. name one opportunity
  3. ask for its saved next step and follow-up tasks
  4. open the deal record beside the answer

questions

what can a salesforce ai agent do without editing my crm?

bring record gaps into your chat. the listed read actions cover opportunity searches, lead details and tasks. these routines return lists without changing records.

can claude update salesforce opportunities?

yes, if you allow that update action. your bot uses the ai you choose. keep reviews read only, or tell it to ask before every record change.

how do i stop the bot making up missing lead information?

make missing values part of the result. define the required fields and forbid guesses. open the linked records in the first reports and compare their fields.
