Metal/integrations

an x assistant that brings the conversation to you

get the product questions buried in your mentions brought to your chat. dusk's twitter toolkit can collect mentions, read list posts and prepare replies.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

it needs your own composio project key and sign-in setup; there's no managed sign-in. keep research read only once connected. the bot brings you questions worth answering, and you review public posts before they go out.

what a bot can do in X

choose whose posts matter before asking for a reply.

a focused source list is easier to review than an open-ended feed. say what kind of question deserves your attention. the bot can gather source posts before suggesting a reply in your Metal chat.

composio, the connection service behind Metal's apps, lists 84 Twitter actions (version 20261101_00, read 12 november 20264). Metal treats 39 as reads, 15 as sends and 30 as changes.

only actions starting with a read word (get, list, search) count as reads, so a read-only bot can't call the rest. a send reaches people or moves money, like add post to bookmarks, and waits for your Allow unless you set a daily amount.

composio has no ready-made sign-in for X. connecting it needs your own composio project key and an auth config of your own, added under Settings, API keys. on dusk cloud, ask us first.

connect X

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick X and sign in on X's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your X.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

x needs your own composio connection. as of 12 november 2026, this toolkit has no composio-managed sign-in. add your own project key and auth config under settings, api keys. then ask the bot which x account it can see before saving a routine.

routines to copy

let the bot do the reading before you decide what deserves a reply. gather mentions, read a specialist list or draft a follow-up in dusk chat. these routines don't publish.

mentions that deserve an answer

every weekday at 11, read mentions for the x account named in your memory notes. bring me direct product questions and reports of broken behaviour. include the original post and why it needs an answer. skip praise without a question. never like, follow or reply.

weekdays at 11:00 · a response queue with original posts and reasons to answer

read a specialist list

every wednesday at 8, read the packaging design x list linked in your memory notes. pick posts about materials or production limits. group repeated ideas and show the posts behind each point. keep opinions labelled as opinions. return the note here without engaging on x.

wednesdays at 8:00 · a topic note with source posts beside each point

prepare a useful follow-up

every friday at 1, read this week's posts from my x account named in your memory notes. suggest one follow-up that answers an unfinished question. put the earlier post and proposed wording here. if no post needs a follow-up, say so. never publish it.

fridays at 13:00 · one proposed follow-up for you to review

what it asks you first

you choose which questions get a public answer. posts and messages ask first by default. a daily sending amount lets them go out within that limit.

careful with Full access: a bot there with no sending rule can send up to 25 a day without asking. give it Ask every time.3

likes, retweets and bookmarks count as sends too. follows, mutes and list changes don't ask first. leave those disabled for research, or add 'ask me before changing anything'.

use posts returned by mention, account-post and list reads for these routines. search and opening an individual post don't work through read only access. dusk counts opening a post as a send.

what it cannot do

  • there's no managed sign-in. your custom composio connection must work before a routine can use x.
  • mention alerts run on your schedule. there's no x event trigger for instant alerts.
  • read only blocks publication and follows. researching a topic doesn't grant permission to engage.
  • keep the reading going while you're away. local routines pause when the computer sleeps or the app closes. dusk cloud keeps the schedule running.

listen before you publish

a useful queue explains why a question matters. ask for the question, original post and reason to answer. competitor monitoring follows the same discipline without turning every mention into a reply.

for social posting, pick a human reviewer. decide which claims need evidence before allowing sends.

find the post everyone is repeating

save yourself ten versions of the same announcement. group repeated claims and keep the original source beside the summary.

a busy thread shows what people are talking about. ask which claims still need evidence, so attention and accuracy stay separate.

if the brief can't tell you why a mention needs you, ask it to leave that mention out.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. finish your custom composio sign-in
  2. confirm the intended x account
  3. ask for a read only mention review
  4. open a source post before drafting its reply

before the listening routine runs

  • the custom connection works
  • the intended account and list are clear
  • only reading actions needed for the job are allowed
  • the output includes original posts and open questions

mistakes people make

planning a routine before sign-in works

finish the custom connection and confirm the account first.

letting the listener follow or mute accounts

leave those actions disabled. keep its job to reading.

treating repeated posts as separate evidence

group the repeats and look for the original source.

questions

can claude automate twitter through Metal?

yes, after the custom connection works. a bot using your chosen ai can call the listed actions. connected apps still calls it twitter. setup comes before scheduling.

will it reply to every mention?

no. this routine only prepares a queue. publication uses a separate send action and asks first by default. keep the researcher read only to block engagement.

can it alert me the second someone mentions me?

schedule the mention review for when you can use it. there is no x event trigger, so it cannot alert you the second a mention appears.
