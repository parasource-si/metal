manus vs Metal: a task service or bots you keep

want a finished deck or a colleague for the next brief too? manus produces research, websites and slides in its cloud computers.1 dusk bots keep lasting jobs, chats and memory.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

manus bundles models into credit plans; dusk separates hosting from your ai bill.25 choose the handoff you want, then try the same small brief before paying for more.

the short version

pick manus if

  • you want a hosted task to produce research, slides or a website
  • you want models inside a credit plan instead of a separate ai bill

pick Metal if

  • you want bots that keep their jobs and memory between tasks
  • you want to choose an ai per bot and decide on hosting separately

compare the budget and the handoff

$20 buys different things here. these are monthly usd figures as of 12 november 2026. manus credits cover work inside its service; dusk cloud buys the machine, with ai billed separately.

a finished task or a lasting role

start with what you want to hand over. manus offers research, websites, slides, images and video.1 its telegram post also describes recurring summaries.3

dusk keeps a bot's job and editable memory between tasks. change its ai and the chats, files and memory stay.4 try an ongoing reviewer if that suits your work.

use the research brief to narrow the task. manus said on 1 september 2026 that it resumed independent operations.12

credits leave one number unknown

run your brief once and watch the credits it eats. as of 12 november 2026, manus lists:2

  • free: 300 daily credits, separate from the monthly pools
  • pro: $20/month and 4,000 monthly credits
  • max: $200/month and 40,000 monthly credits, then pay as you go

that gives you the allowance, but only a trial tells you what your report consumes.

dusk's bill has two parts: the machine and ai. personal local use is free; cloud plus is $20/month and pro $50/month. your provider bills ai.58 work needs a license; cloud pro includes one for one person.811 put every line in the cost guide.

make the missing answers visible

get the unanswered questions before they reach the client. give both products a few proposals and your requirements. ask for file evidence beside each claim, with missing answers left open. read the claims against the files.

for dusk, start with attachments. inspect notes under memory, or turn off let this bot use memory.10 ask for the source file even when the bot remembers the context. see manus alternatives for other ways to hand over the work.

how we checked

Metal is based on Dusk Bot. we haven't run the same brief through both. this compares manus's product and pricing as of 12 november 2026 with dusk docs.

we cannot price an identical report in manus credits or name a winner for research accuracy and finished files.

bring the same files and questions to each trial. a different brief cannot settle the comparison.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. choose two short proposals and a requirement list
  2. ask for a comparison with evidence beside each claim
  3. trace two claims to the files and note actual usage before expanding the task

questions

how many free credits does manus ai give?

300 a day, as of 12 november 2026. free includes manus 2.0 lite, 3 tasks at once and 2 automations. that does not tell you how many of your tasks will finish.2

what should i compare before switching from manus?

test the work you actually repeat. compare the same narrow brief, sources, usage and where files live. we have not run that comparison, so we cannot name a quality or cost winner.

can i message manus from telegram?

yes. its launch post describes linking telegram and getting recurring summaries there.3 dusk has its own desktop and paired phone interface. store phone apps are still on the way.7

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
