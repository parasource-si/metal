poke vs Metal: where you text your assistant

pick where you'll actually text it. poke works like a contact in apple messages, telegram, whatsapp or rcs.12 dusk uses its own desktop and paired phone interface, with separate bots for different jobs.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

poke runs in the cloud. dusk bots live on your computer, server or dusk cloud.37 pick where you'll text it first. the rest follows.

the short version

pick poke if

  • you want your usual messaging app. text through apple messages, telegram, whatsapp or rcs.
  • you want cloud and ai together. both come inside its service plan.

pick Metal if

  • you have separate responsibilities to hand over. each bot gets a job and memory.
  • you want to choose the home and ai. each bot uses the model you pick.

start with the place you will actually use it

a similar price can buy a different setup. these monthly usd prices and channels are as of 12 november 2026. the plans do not promise equal ai work.

use the conversation you will come back to

if you want a familiar contact, poke fits that habit. it directly supports existing messaging apps.2 a separate bot workspace adds little if you only want a quick question or reminder. assistants you text compares that choice.

dusk pairing opens a phone browser you can add to your home screen. it has no notifications today. store apps are still on the way.6

if you depend on a ping to notice completed work, take that limit seriously.

a paired phone does not move the bot

get the phone reply even after you've shut the laptop. poke already runs as a cloud agent.3 a Metal bot on your computer needs it on with the app running.6

dusk cloud hosts your bots on your own unshared machine. pair your phone there and it works with the laptop off. ai usage still goes on the provider's bill.79

choose the home before adding more work. the personal agent guide walks through that decision.

add roles only when they earn their keep

give each bot a job worth keeping separate. a planner and a draft reviewer can each keep their own memory and ai.8

try: "every monday at 9, read upcoming deadlines in my writing-project folder. ask which commitment matters most this week. return the question here; change nothing."

if a reminder contact is enough, poke's reminders and schedules may fit better.1 poke alternatives covers the other choices.

start dusk app access read only.12 for jobs that need writes, include "ask me before you change anything"; app edits can run without asking.11

how we checked

try the habit you want to keep. poke's own pages don't name its models or explain how it asks before acting. ask about those details when you try it.

mistakes people make

questions

can poke message me first?

yes. as of 12 november 2026, its homepage lists proactive messages, reminders and scheduled tasks. pro lists background automations.1

how can an agent answer my phone with my laptop closed?

the bot needs an awake home elsewhere. poke runs in the cloud.3 for dusk, pair to dusk cloud. pairing to your computer still needs that computer on and the app running.67

is poke joining cognition?

yes, according to its current announcement. cognition welcomed the interaction company on 23 july 2026 and said users could continue as before. poke still showed the joining banner on 12 november 2026.13
