lindy vs Metal: shared work, credits and control

**lindy puts the helper where your team already talks.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk gives each job its own home.** lindy handles email, meetings, leads and reports through tools such as slack and email.1 its team plan pools credits and charges per active user. dusk separates bot jobs, memory and ai choices, with ai usage and hosting billed separately.25

the short version

pick lindy if

  • you want one hosted service for shared work. reach it through slack, email and text.
  • you prefer a team credit pool. choose models within that service.

pick Metal if

  • you want distinct bot roles. choose each bot’s ai and app permissions.
  • you want to try jobs before choosing hosting. start on your own computer and move later.

price the routine your team will actually run. monthly usd prices are as of 12 november 2026. lindy pools credits; how far they go depends on the work.

try the approval on a job with consequences

find out where the bot stops before it touches a customer’s work. lindy says actions with outside impact need approval.1 ask it to show that pause on the job your team needs.

in dusk, start with connected apps read only. for writes, say "ask me before you change anything". command and file approval levels are separate controls.910

then ask lindy what counts as an active user. lindy alternatives covers the credit pool and pricing in more detail.

questions

does lindy have a free plan?

you get a trial, not a renewing free allowance. as of 12 november 2026, lindy lists $50 in credits for 7 days. team starts at $29.99 monthly per active user.2

can i text lindy?

yes, its pricing lists imessage. it also lists slack, email and phone calls.2 dusk uses a paired phone browser today; store apps are still on the way.7

is lindy better than openclaw for a business owner?

pick who you want handling the upkeep first. lindy is hosted; openclaw runs on your computer or server with no first-party hosted tier.13 then try the same small job and compare the complete usage bill.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
