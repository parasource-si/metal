perplexity comet alternatives beyond your browser

want help with the tabs in front of you? comet fits that job. it researches and acts on pages inside your browser.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk gives named bots their own ongoing work, files, memory and routines, with a separate browser and desktop available. choose dusk when you want the helper to keep its own workspace while you keep yours.

the short version

pick comet if

  • you want help inside your browser. comet builds its assistant into chromium.
  • you want familiar browser tools. import your setup and use most chrome extensions.
  • you want models within one service. perplexity handles the routing through its plans.

pick Metal if

  • you want a separate work area. give the bot its own browser and desktop.
  • you want to watch and step in. use screen, take control, then hand control back.
  • you want the job to stay with a bot. keep its files, memory and ongoing conversations together.

browser assistant versus a bot with a computer

budget for the assistant you need after the download. as of 12 november 2026, comet’s free plan leaves browser agent queries out. we did no browser-speed or task-success test.

which browser do you want it working in?

comet helps in the browser you use every day. it is a chromium browser for research and page actions.1 import another browser’s setup and keep most chrome extensions.1

dusk keeps the bot’s work together. each cloud bot gets a desktop; a server bot can have a private one there. using your own mac screen is a deliberate choice.6

if you want the bot to have its own working area, read ai that controls a computer. you can keep your everyday browser as it is.

price the assistant, not the download

comet’s free download gets you the browser. agent queries need a paid plan. as of 12 november 2026, free has no browser agent queries; pro and max have monthly limits.4 read the current pro offer before subscribing.

dusk’s personal desktop app is free, with ai billed by your provider. cloud hosting is $20 monthly for plus or $50 for pro, with ai separate.89

compare the bill for the setup you will actually use. for a signed-in ai in dusk, read your provider’s current terms too.

a web page can contain instructions aimed at the bot

a page can try to give your bot orders. brave disclosed an indirect prompt-injection flaw in comet page summarisation on 20 august 2025.13 its current fix status is unverified here.

keep page content separate from your instructions in either tool. in dusk, watch with screen, take control for sign-ins and stop the bot when needed.6

start with a reading task that ends before submitting forms. the computer-control safety guide explains the access choices.

questions

is perplexity comet free?

the browser download is free. as of 12 november 2026, the free plan includes no browser agent queries. pro and max have monthly limits.4

is comet safe for agent work?

keep the first job to reading public pages. brave disclosed a prompt-injection flaw on 20 august 2025.13 we have not retested it; its current fix status remains unverified here.

can a Metal bot use a browser separate from mine?

yes, on its own computer. dusk cloud gives each bot a desktop. your own server can host a private desktop too. watch with screen and take control when needed. your mac screen is a separate choice.6
