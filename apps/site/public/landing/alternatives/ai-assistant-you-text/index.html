ai assistants you text: choose the channel and the computer

texting your assistant from the couch should start with the chat app you already open. poke feels like a contact; it's joining cognition, announced 23 july 2026.3 openclaw and hermes connect chat apps to agents you can run.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

genspark claw offers cloud or local work. dusk has its own app. choose where to text, then which computer stays awake.

the short version

pick a messaging-app assistant if

  • stay beside your contacts. you want imessage, whatsapp or telegram without another inbox.
  • use the channel you already open. pick a tool whose hosting and setup you are willing to keep running.
  • keep one place to talk. a familiar messaging app matters more to you than a dedicated bot team.

pick Metal if

  • give each bot a job. you want separate names, memories and conversations.
  • choose the ai per bot. switch models while keeping its chats.
  • pair to your own machine. use your computer, server or dusk cloud through the dusk phone browser.

if imessage is the requirement, start there

want a contact in imessage? start with poke or openclaw. poke lists apple messages, whatsapp, telegram and rcs.1 openclaw also lists imessage.4 compare their setup and hosting before choosing.

dusk uses its own app. pair its browser version on iphone or android and add it to your home screen. store apps are on the way. the browser version sends no notifications.12

if imessage decides it for you, read the poke comparison.

whatsapp and telegram leave you another decision

the chat channel is only half the choice. openclaw, hermes and genspark claw list whatsapp and telegram.479

  • openclaw runs on your machine or server4
  • hermes agent adds an optional cloud agent on paid nous plans7
  • genspark claw offers a dedicated cloud computer or local desktop mode; connect chat apps under channels9

local mode still needs your computer on. check your model provider's current terms before relying on its sign-in.

give the first text a clear boundary

start with a job you can inspect. try: "help me plan a weekly review of this project folder. show the schedule first. ask before changing anything."

in dusk, a routine returns a card with next runs, edit, pause and undo.15 read it before relying on the timing.

local routines stop when the app closes or the computer sleeps. a miss under 12 hours runs once when you return; older runs skip.15

mistakes people make

choosing by the phone screenshot

find which machine does the work. a chat bubble can hide a sleeping laptop.

expecting browser notifications from dusk

the current paired phone browser sends none. choose with that limit in mind.
