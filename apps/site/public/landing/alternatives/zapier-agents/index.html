a zapier agents alternative you talk to in its own app

keep the automation that works; move the job that's still a pain. zapier agents acts across a large app catalogue, by request or trigger.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk gives you named bots with separate memories, in a desktop app with local or hosted homes. zapier agents activities and zap automation tasks use different allowances. find out what your job spends before comparing bills.

the short version

pick zapier agents if

  • you need an unusual app. its catalogue lists 9,000+ apps.
  • you want cloud triggers. no computer for you to keep running.
  • you want agents calling agents. that handoff exists inside zapier.

pick Metal if

  • you want separate bot conversations. each bot keeps its own job and memory.
  • you want to choose each bot's ai. change it later as the job changes.
  • you want to watch the work. bots can have a separate browser and desktop.

an activity and a zap task spend different allowances. prices are as of 12 november 2026. compare the charge for your actual process; a dusk chat uses another billing setup.

activities are not zap tasks

budget from agents activities, or you'll buy the wrong allowance. as of 12 november 2026, agents free has 400 monthly activities. pro has 1,500, billed at $400 yearly and shown as $33.33 monthly. enterprise pricing is custom.2

the automation free plan's 100 tasks belong to another allowance.2 zapier's pricing doesn't explain exactly which agent steps spend an activity. ask zapier to show the accounting for your process.

gumloop uses credits and orchestration fees; n8n bills workflow executions. compare a finished job against its bill, rather than treating those units as equal.

find the action behind the app name

one missing action can strand the whole job. zapier lists 9,000+ apps;1 dusk lists 60+.6 an unusual tool is a good reason to try zapier first.

write down each read, draft or change your process needs. try the essential operation before rebuilding everything around it.

in dusk, limit the bot to reads or exact actions.6 keep the first trial read only. if the job needs writes, put "ask me before you change anything" in the routine.11

try the friday file review

get the weekly file roundup out of your head. ask: "every friday at 3, read your folder's files from this week. list filenames with one line about each. change nothing." bots keep their work in their own folders, alongside chats and memory.46

moving because you want phone chat? dusk uses its own app and paired phone browser. store apps are on the way.5

see assistants you text for messaging-app choices. read your ai provider's current terms before choosing the ai for your Metal bot.

how we checked

try the operation your process depends on. we read zapier's agents and pricing pages against dusk docs on 12 november 2026. Metal is based on Dusk Bot.

we didn't run matching tasks or measure a real process's activities. we haven't tested each connection either. the catalogue tells you where to start looking; your trial tells you whether the job works.

keep the old workflow until the new one finishes the job you actually need.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. choose one existing workflow
  2. write down its essential app action
  3. try that exact action in the alternative
  4. compare billing units before moving it

before you switch a working process

  • every required action exists, beyond the app name
  • you know which steps spend an activity
  • the annual commitment fits your budget
  • the result arrives where you will read it

how much does zapier agents cost?

as of 12 november 2026: 400 monthly activities on free. pro has 1,500 for $400 yearly, shown as $33.33 monthly. enterprise is custom. these are agents plans, separate from automation plans.2

will an agent run while my laptop is closed?

zapier says its agents run in the cloud.1 local dusk routines need an awake computer and open app. dusk cloud keeps them running on time.89

is a zapier task the same as an agents activity?

they are separate allowances.2 ask zapier to show the bill for your exact process before comparing plans.
