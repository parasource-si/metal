genspark alternatives for people who want ongoing bots

keep genspark if its editors are what save you the afternoon. it brings slides, sheets, documents and media tools together with claw, an assistant in chat apps.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk fits ongoing jobs with separate memory and ai choices. both offer local and hosted routes. choose around the work you keep doing, then add up the computer and ai bills.1234

the short version

pick genspark if

  • you make things in its workspace. slides, sheets, documents and media tools belong together for you.
  • you want claw in existing chats. choose its cloud computer or desktop local mode.

pick Metal if

  • you want helpers with distinct jobs. name each bot and pick its ai.
  • you want to choose where memory lives. pay the ai provider separately from the computer host.

separate the workspace from claw's computer

claw’s cheap computer still needs credits to do the work. prices are as of 12 november 2026. the cloud starting rate is promotional; read the current offer before paying.

what would you miss if you left?

if you spend your day in genspark’s editors, keep them. slides, spreadsheets, documents and media tools sit alongside its agents.1 dusk does not have those same editors. manus alternatives is another comparison if finished deliverables drive the choice.

dusk is for work that stays with a bot: its job, conversations and memory. change the ai later without starting over.10

try attaching two planning notes and asking where their dates conflict. you get a file review in chat.9 ai teammates explains how to keep that role going.

try local mode before paying for another cloud

claw already works without a cloud computer subscription. its macos and windows desktop app has local mode.1 if hosting cost is the whole problem, try that before switching.

local mode can reach your files. genspark’s help centre suggests choosing a workspace folder.1 in dusk, attach the files or choose a project folder, and keep connected apps read only for inspection.9 give either agent a clear working area.

the cheap computer is only one part of the bill

the computer fee is the start of the calculation. as of 12 november 2026, genspark’s monthly prices include:

  • plus from $24.99, with 10,000 monthly credits
  • pro from $249.99, with 125,000 monthly credits
  • claw cloud from a promotional $9.99 monthly, charged separately12

claw jobs spend those shared credits too.2 run your recurring job and look at its credit use before calling the computer cheap.

dusk cloud charges for the computer; your ai provider bills the work.45 if that ai allowance runs out, wait for the reset or change ai.10

put both totals into the agent cost guide. that gives you a bill for your job instead of a contest between headline prices.

how we checked

compare a job you do every week before moving your workspace. Metal is based on Dusk Bot. this comparison uses genspark’s workspace and claw information from 12 november 2026. we did not run matching decks, reports or scheduled jobs.

70+ models tells you the menu, not which one will fix your deck. shared credits and the promotional computer rate also need a real workload before you can price them. renewal pricing remains unknown.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. name the genspark tool you use most
  2. choose one small task with files you can inspect
  3. try that same task with one Metal
  4. compare corrections needed and the separate bills

cancel around a job you can replace, not around a cheaper number on a pricing card.

mistakes people make

reading $9.99 as the whole bill

that is a promotional computer rate. claw work consumes credits too.

replacing editors with a chat and hoping it feels the same

test your actual creation task. keep genspark if its workspace is what helps.

questions

is genspark claw cloud only?

no, it has desktop local mode too. that route needs no cloud computer subscription. choose its working folder carefully because local mode can reach files.1

are claw’s scheduled tasks free?

scheduled runs consume credits. a free workspace allowance has limits, and choosing a cloud computer adds its own fee.12

does dusk offer ai teammates?

yes, bots hand work over with mentions in group chats. use that for ongoing roles with separate jobs and memory.11 keep genspark for its workspace editors if those are what you need.
