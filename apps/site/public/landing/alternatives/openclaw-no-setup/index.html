openclaw setup without the terminal

get to your first reply without opening a terminal. openclaw's desktop apps install the pieces for you.1 you still choose an ai and keep its computer running.2 Metal offers named bots and optional hosting later.34 try the app and sign-in first, then decide who handles upkeep.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

we haven't timed first replies in either.

the short version

pick choose openclaw’s desktop route when if

  • you want openclaw’s chat channels. try its installer if the terminal was your only obstacle.
  • you want software you can change. openclaw uses the mit license; you look after the machine.

pick Metal if

  • you want a named bot with a clear job. add separate jobs when the first one works.
  • you want hosting as a later option. move chats, memory and routines to a dusk cloud computer.

get one reply before connecting your working life

try the current installer before giving up on openclaw. its docs call windows hub the easiest desktop path. terminal instructions remain an option, not a requirement.1

in dusk, use new bot, give it one job and ask one question. personal desktop use needs no dusk account.3610

leave work apps disconnected until that first answer works. it is easier to spot a sign-in problem before you add several permissions.

decide who keeps the computer running

someone has to keep the morning job running. openclaw runs on your computer or server; its project offers no hosted tier.2 third-party offers are a separate choice in managed openclaw hosting.

dusk cloud hosts the computer and backs it up daily. move bots there with chats, memory and routines.4

staying local costs less in dusk. routines stop during sleep, though. read where agents run with the lid shut before counting on an overnight job.

the setup ends when you know what you own

choose who gets the call when it breaks next month. the installer leaves three decisions with you.

  • where the assistant runs
  • which ai answers
  • who handles the machine

openclaw's desktop route handles installation. you own the running setup.12

dusk hosting doesn't include ai usage. when the plan runs out, the bot tells you: wait for the reset or switch ai.610 read your provider's current terms. the broader alternatives help if upkeep was the real concern.
