gumloop alternatives when you want to give a job in a message

keep the builder if you like building. text a bot if you want to hand over the job. gumloop already has chat, agents and browser work.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk gives you named bots with standing jobs and a choice of where they live. choose by how you want to work, then add up both the hosting and ai bills.

the short version

pick gumloop if

  • you want a no-code builder with 400+ connectors for agents and workflows
  • you want a hosted browser with saved logins and up to 25 agent chats at once

pick Metal if

  • you want each named bot's job, chats and memory together
  • you want to start on your computer and pay the ai provider separately
  • you want to describe a small repeating job and adjust it in conversation

what changes if you switch

put the handoff beside the bill. prices as of 12 november 2026. compare fees and credits before paying; the table covers features, not measured reply quality.

chat and browser work are already there

gumloop already gives you agents and a browser. it saves browser logins and lists chat, slack, teams, imessage and email on its homepage.1

use its builder when you want to lay out the process. try dusk when you want to give a bot a standing job and adjust it in conversation.

if the workflow is what you enjoy shaping, compare n8n and zapier agents too.

the credit terms belong beside the price

price the job, not just the $37 plan. as of 12 november 2026, gumloop pro advertises 20,000 monthly credits. here's the split:2

7,400 regular credits
+ 12,600 bonus credits
= 20,000 credits
  • standard orchestration fee: 8%
  • unused credits expire
  • bring your own api keys: model-use credits fall to zero, but the fee rises to 16%

dusk bills hosting separately; your provider bills ai. personal desktop use is free, while work needs a license.5612 add both bills and read your provider's current sign-in terms. we haven't measured what the same job costs in each.

test the handoff on five files

give it a small job before rebuilding your workflow. try: "list the five newest files in this folder and explain each. leave the files alone." compare the list with the folder.

dusk accepts attached documents. output lives in the bot's folder or a project folder you choose.8 if that works, consider a routine.

local routines stop when dusk closes or your computer sleeps. use an awake machine or dusk cloud for work that must run on time.9

how we checked

Metal is based on Dusk Bot. we haven't raced it against gumloop. this comparison uses gumloop's homepage and pricing as of 12 november 2026, alongside dusk docs.

we have no matching-job measurements for speed, accuracy or cost per result. try your own small job before moving a workflow.

keep the old workflow until the replacement handles a small real job you can check.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. pick one folder with five files you know
  2. ask a Metal bot for the file list and one line about each
  3. read its output beside the files, then inspect the usage charge

questions

is gumloop free or paid?

paid, after a trial. as of 12 november 2026, pro starts at $37/month after 14 days. credits and an orchestration fee apply. no ongoing free tier appears.2

gumloop vs n8n: which should i compare first?

compare how they charge for your actual process. gumloop uses credits and orchestration fees. n8n charges per workflow run. see the n8n comparison for its prices.210

do i need a workflow builder for a weekly report?

a few files and a short report can start with a message. test a narrow bot instruction. use a builder when you want control over the sequence. compare the output with the files before scheduling it.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
