manus alternatives when you want a different ai bill

need a finished deck, or a bot that knows next week's job? manus bundles cloud tasks, models and credits.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk keeps ongoing bots on the ai you choose, with your provider billing usage.123

as of 12 november 2026, manus is independent again after the meta deal unwound. old meta-owned descriptions miss the second half.10

the short version

pick manus if

  • you want a finished deliverable. manus packages research, websites, slides and media in one cloud product.
  • you prefer its models and credits. pro also offers manus flex for your own keys.

pick Metal if

  • you want bots you keep. give each one a job, chats, memory and its own ai.
  • you want a separate ai bill. start with personal desktop use and pay your provider for usage.

a task allowance or a team you keep

the same $20 buys credits here and hosting there. prices and ownership are as of 12 november 2026. compare what you need delivered before comparing the allowances.

daily credits and monthly credits are different

the free daily allowance won't tell you what pro gets done. manus free lists 300 credits a day; pro lists 4,000 a month. pro allows 20 tasks at once, versus 3 on free.1

try a job you actually need and see how much allowance it uses. dusk leaves ai usage with your provider.2 free personal desktop use still needs that ai.

compare the recurring job, your existing ai allowance and hosting needs. the agent cost guide separates those costs.

the ownership story has a second half

read the september update before trusting an old meta headline. manus announced the meta deal on 29 december 2025.4

the global times reports that china's ndrc ordered it unwound in april 2026. manus resumed independent operations on 1 september, led by its founding team.10

the report also describes backup notices and possible data loss during separation. read current account notices before relying on old history. the ownership story matters most if your work is still in that account.

a deliverable or a bot you keep

keep manus on the shortlist if you want its creation package. it offers websites, slides and media alongside research.4 dusk's different focus is a bot with an ongoing job.

that bot can keep several threads sharing memory and switch ai without losing chats or files.5 ai teammates explains the setup. manus versus Metal covers the direct choice.

try one narrow recurring job first. inspect its early runs before handing over more.

how we checked

use the published offer to choose your trial. we read manus pricing and product pages against dusk docs on 12 november 2026. Metal is based on Dusk Bot.

we haven't run identical creation or research jobs through both, so we can't turn credits into a price for your finished work.

the ownership update comes from a press report. the older acquisition announcement shows how the story started.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. name one job you want to repeat
  2. write down the output you will accept
  3. decide whether you need hosting or already have ai access
  4. try that job before comparing monthly allowances

make it finish the job before deciding the credits are good value.

mistakes people make

comparing the two $20 prices as identical bundles

list hosting, model usage and allowance separately before picking a plan.

calling manus meta-owned from an old article

read the september 2026 independence report alongside the original announcement.

questions

manus ai: how many free credits do i get?

300 credits daily, as of 12 november 2026. free also lists manus 2.0 lite, 3 simultaneous tasks and 2 automations. try your job to see how far those credits go.1

what are people switching to instead of manus?

switch for the job you want to keep doing. dusk fits ongoing bots and separate ai billing.23 manus may fit better for finished slides or websites.4 we have no survey of people leaving.

is manus still owned by meta?

it is independent again, according to the september report. operations resumed on 1 september 2026 after the ordered unwind. the december 2025 announcement covers the earlier deal. read both for the position as of 12 november 2026.410
