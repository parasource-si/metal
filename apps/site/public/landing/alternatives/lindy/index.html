lindy ai alternatives for a smaller team of bots

keep lindy if your team lives in its work channels and shared credit pool. it offers a hosted assistant for email, meetings, leads and reports.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk gives you bots with separate jobs, memories and ai choices. the provider bill stays separate too. compare the full work cost: dusk’s free desktop use covers personal jobs; business use needs a license.1234

the short version

pick lindy if

  • you want it where work happens. lindy lists slack, email, imessage and calls.
  • your team wants a shared credit pool. its integration list may also cover a tool you need.

pick Metal if

  • you want distinct jobs. each bot has its own chats and memory.
  • you want to choose the ai. start with personal jobs on your computer and keep provider billing separate.

the work plan behind the headline price

count active people before comparing the headline price. figures are as of 12 november 2026. lindy pools credits across the team; dusk bills ai separately and needs a license for work.

try the tool your team cannot work without

one missing work app can cost more than the subscription. lindy lists 3,000+ integrations, model selection and scheduled routines. you can reach it through slack and email.12

dusk lists 60+ connected apps and lets you add trusted mcp servers.9 try the exact job before moving your team:

  • connect the account your team uses
  • run the read, draft or change you need
  • send the result to the place someone will read it

lindy versus dusk gives the direct comparison. ai employees helps narrow the job before you buy.

find out how fast your job spends credits

price the emails you need done, then ask what they spend. as of 12 november 2026, lindy team starts at $29.99 per active user monthly.2

  • the starting allowance is 3,000 credits
  • credits pool across the team and do not roll over
  • top-ups cost $10 per 1,000 credits

ask how your actual job spends that pool.

dusk’s personal desktop app is free. business use needs a work license. cloud pro is $50 monthly and includes one person’s license.348 add your ai provider’s bill before calling either option cheaper.

test the handoff before building a department

one useful result beats a roster of impressive titles. attach the files for a bounded job. ask for a result in chat, beside the files it makes. local work lives in its folder or your chosen project folder.9

start connected apps read only. ordinary edits do not automatically ask first. if you allow writes later, say “ask me before you change anything”.11

for a small business, name the person reading each run. routine history shows what ran; pause stops the next one. that person still needs to judge the answer.10

how we checked

we compared the offers, not finished-task performance. Metal is based on Dusk Bot. we read lindy’s homepage and pricing on 12 november 2026 alongside dusk’s docs.

we did not run identical jobs or measure success. each product counts integrations its own way. lindy’s page uses inconsistent plan names, so we left computer-use features out of the tier comparison.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. pick a recurring job your team already does
  2. try its essential app action in both products
  3. count the active people who need it
  4. compare credit use, ai charges and licensing for that job

try the integration your team cannot work without before counting the other thousands.
