poke ai alternatives when you want an app for your bots

poke suits an assistant beside your contacts. it works in imessage and other chat apps without an install. Metal suits separate bots in their own app, with an ai you choose per bot.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

poke bundles models; dusk separates ai and hosting bills. as of 12 november 2026, poke says it is joining cognition. existing users can continue as before.1234

the short version

pick poke if

  • you want apple messages, telegram, whatsapp or rcs as the conversation's home
  • you want an always-on hosted assistant with models inside its plan

pick Metal if

  • you want separate jobs, memories and conversations for several bots
  • you want to choose each bot's ai and the machine holding its chats and files

the familiar contact is a good reason to stay

another app can be one app too many. poke works in messaging services you already use, including apple messages.1 if texting a familiar contact solves it, stay there. the texting-assistant comparison covers more choices.

dusk gives each bot a name, job, chats and memory.5 keep your personal helper apart from your work bot, and choose the ai for each. that organisation is the reason to switch; we haven't compared reply quality.

the phone works only if its host does

text from the couch with the laptop shut. poke is an always-on cloud agent, according to cognition.4

in dusk, pair your phone to where your bots live:

  • your computer: keep it on with dusk running
  • dusk cloud: your computer can be off
  • current phone version: browser and home-screen shortcut, without notifications; store apps are on the way67

read poke versus Metal if phone access decides the choice.

one bundle or two bills

look past the sticker price to the work it buys. poke bundles models; ultra adds pay-as-you-go usage beyond credits. poke doesn't say which models you get or how far a credit goes.2

dusk separates the machine from the ai. personal local use costs nothing to dusk; your provider still bills ai. a hosted computer adds another charge.37

choose the personal agent whose bill you can follow as the work grows.

what the cognition news actually says

existing poke users can carry on as before. cognition's announcement says so. it announced the interaction company's move on 23 july 2026; poke still says it is joining cognition.4 company status as of 12 november 2026.

how we checked

we build Metal. we haven't sent the same job to both. prices and company status reflect 12 november 2026.

poke doesn't name its models or publish a usable credit-to-task rate. we cannot price an identical job or rank delivery reliability.

if one contact in messages solves the problem, a team of bots may be more than you need.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. write down whether the assistant must live in imessage
  2. if trying dusk, make one bot with one job
  3. pair your phone and try a message with the laptop shut

questions

what is the lowest poke ai price?

$0 to start, as of 12 november 2026. pro costs $19/month and ultra $199/month. ultra adds pay-as-you-go usage beyond credits. look at the current allowance before scheduling work.2

how are people running agents from their phone?

the phone reaches work running elsewhere. poke uses existing chat apps. dusk pairs its phone browser to your computer or cloud. use cloud if your computer needs to be off.16

can i choose the exact ai in poke?

poke doesn't name the exact models. pro and ultra describe frontier models. dusk lets you pick and change an ai per bot, under that provider's terms and limits.25
