sintra alternatives when you want to define the jobs yourself

buy the role you need this week, not the whole imaginary department. sintra packages ready-made helpers with ai included.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk lets you write the jobs and choose each bot’s ai separately. try the task that keeps getting put off, then compare the bill. sintra’s listed rates are promotional; the payment term and renewal price matter as much as the monthly headline.

the short version

pick sintra if

  • the packaged roles suit you. you want helpers for the business areas sintra lists.
  • you want ai inside one plan. you prefer that to a separate provider bill.
  • you want store phone apps today. sintra links ios and android apps.

pick Metal if

  • you want to write the job yourself. name a bot and change its responsibility as you learn.
  • you want separate memories and ai choices. each bot keeps its own.
  • you want a choice of computer. use your own, your server or dusk cloud.

put the commitment beside the sale price. these offers appeared on 12 november 2026. check the term, total payment and renewal price before buying.

buy the job, not the staff count

a helper roster is useful only if it does work you need. sintra lists social media, seo, support, email marketing and assistance.1 match those roles to your actual week.

in dusk you write the job yourself.3 try: “read this draft against the attached voice guide. flag passages that sound unlike us. do not publish.” attach the guide in chat.9

that task has a result you can inspect. “run marketing” has a title and a lot of unanswered questions. ai employees helps draw that boundary.

read the term before the discount

a low monthly figure can mean a longer commitment. as of 12 november 2026, sintra shows these sale and list prices:1

  • one month: $48.50/month, list $97
  • three months: $23.60/month, list $59
  • twelve months: $15.60/month, list $52
  • individual helpers: $39/month

the package includes the full helper set and 250 monthly credits. that does not tell us how many usable drafts the credits buy. read the renewal rate and total payment before committing.

dusk’s desktop app is free for personal use. work needs a license; optional cloud hosting costs $20 or $50 a month. ai usage stays on your provider bill.567 read your provider’s current terms too. we have not measured costs for identical tasks.

decide what each bot should know

give the writer and reviewer the same brief. each Metal bot keeps its own memory. group chats let bots hand work over with mentions or a leader. learning from groups is optional per bot.31011

attach the sources each job needs. ai teammates explains that setup. sintra’s pages leave collaboration unclear, so try a handoff before buying it for that reason. relevance ai is another workforce option; read its current pricing.

keep connected-app trials read only. ordinary edits do not automatically ask. if you permit writes, say “ask me before you change anything”. sending has its own setting.912

how we checked

try your real job before buying the helper package. we read sintra’s homepage and pricing on 12 november 2026. those pages did not explain helper collaboration clearly enough for a fair comparison, so we left it out.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. write down a task you keep putting off
  2. match it to a sintra helper or a dusk job
  3. read the current payment term and renewal rate
  4. try the result before adding more roles

questions

are sintra’s advertised prices permanent?

they are promotional offers as of 12 november 2026. one-, three- and twelve-month terms differ. check the current price and renewal terms before paying.1

does sintra include ai in its plan?

yes, as of 12 november 2026. sintra says it uses claude 4.5 sonnet. you do not bring an ai key.1

does dusk have a phone app like sintra?

dusk’s store apps are still on the way. sintra links ios and android apps.2 today, pair your phone and add dusk’s browser version to the home screen. that version sends no notifications.8
