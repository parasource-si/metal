openclaw hosting or a different cloud bot app?

get your weekends back without giving up openclaw. managed hosting keeps the assistant you chose and hands server care to someone else.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk cloud runs a different product: dusk bots, chats, memory and routines. choose the assistant first, then compare renewal rates and ai charges. the opening rate is only part of what you'll pay.1234

the short version

pick managed openclaw if

  • keep openclaw's channels and skills. let a host handle server care.
  • pay for upkeep rather than a product move. you already want the open-source project.

pick Metal if

  • keep named dusk bots. each has its own chats on your dedicated hosted computer.
  • move existing dusk work off your laptop. bring chats, memory and routines to dusk cloud.

read the term beside the rate

$4.99 monthly asks for $119.76 up front. hostinger's promotion covers a 24-month term and renews at $11.99 monthly. those terms are from 12 november 2026; region and date can change them.1

elestio starts at $16 monthly, including compute, storage, bandwidth, updates and backups.2

dusk cloud plus costs $20 monthly or $200 yearly. pro costs $50 monthly or $500 yearly and includes one person's work license.4 ai usage costs extra.5 use the cost tool with both bills in mind.

managed doesn't choose what the agent may touch

pay the host to patch the server; choose the agent's reach yourself. hostinger and elestio describe handling security and backups.12 openclaw's skills and plugins still need your judgement.6 use the safety guide before adding them.

in dusk, start a new bot with read only access.7 for app edits later, put 'ask me before changing anything' into the routine.7

choose the assistant before the server

if openclaw fits, hosting may be all you need. its desktop installers also reduce setup work.8

dusk cloud moves existing dusk chats, memory and routines.4 moving from openclaw means rebuilding the jobs, instructions and schedules in dusk; there's no importer.

look at the finished job, even when someone else handles the machine. server backups cover the server; don't count on them to restore every connected service.

is openclaw hosting free?
