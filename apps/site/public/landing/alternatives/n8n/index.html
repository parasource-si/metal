an n8n alternative for people who do not want to build workflows

if you enjoy drawing the steps, keep n8n. its visual canvas gives you the sequence to build and inspect.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

if you would rather describe the job, dusk gives you a bot to steer and a result to review. both can run on servers. choose how much process you want to build and maintain yourself.

the short version

pick n8n if

  • draw the process. you want a visual canvas for multi-step workflows and agents.
  • host the community edition. you accept its sustainable use license.
  • pay per execution. that meter suits you better than counting steps or users.

pick Metal if

  • describe the job once. make routines through chat.
  • keep context with a bot. its job, conversations and memory stay together.
  • try the desktop app first. decide on hosting once the work needs it.

a workflow canvas beside a bot conversation

n8n starter is 20 eur a month, with the year paid up front. prices are as of 12 november 2026. executions measure workflow runs; your finished job can take more than one.

first decide who should spell out the steps

own the sequence when the sequence matters. n8n represents multi-step workflows and ai agents visually.1 inspect the steps you built, then read the result.

a dusk routine starts with the job: "every friday at 4, list files added to this project folder this week. give one line on each."78

try a small report first. gumloop offers another approach if the workflow shape matters most.

execution pricing is useful, but not the whole budget

budget for the job you will repeat. as of 12 november 2026, n8n bills per workflow execution, rather than per step or user.2

  • starter: 20 eur/month, billed annually; 2,500 executions
  • pro: 50 eur/month, billed annually; 10,000 executions
  • business: 667 eur/month, billed annually; 40,000 executions; self-hosted only
  • community edition: free to self-host2

n8n cloud also includes ai model access and assistant credits.2 an execution and a dusk message count different things. price a real run before deciding which bill is smaller.

a server does not turn one tool into the other

choose who keeps the machine running. n8n offers cloud and self-hosting. dusk offers your computer, your server or dusk cloud.24 openclaw gives you another route to compare.

local dusk routines stop when the app closes or the computer sleeps. misses under 12 hours run once when you return; older ones skip.7 scheduled work needs an awake machine.

start connected apps read only. for later edits, write "ask me before you change anything"; ordinary app changes use that boundary, rather than the sending setting.810 read your ai provider's current terms before relying on its sign-in.10

how we checked

we compared the offers, not matching test runs. n8n's repository and pricing were checked on 12 november 2026. dusk details come from our docs.

we didn't build the same workflow in both. use the comparison to choose the kind of setup you want, then test your actual job.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. write one repeated job in a single sentence
  2. list the steps you must control exactly
  3. list the parts that need judgement
  4. choose a canvas or bot trial around that split

mistakes people make

comparing executions with chat messages

they measure different things. price the actual repeated job instead.

treating a free local install as an always-on service

the machine still needs to stay awake. include hosting and upkeep in your choice.

questions

is there an n8n alternative for non-technical people?

try dusk for a small job in plain words. choose n8n if you need its visual workflow canvas. decide whether you want to control each step or review the finished work.

where do your agents actually run when your laptop is closed?

on a different machine that stays on. n8n offers its cloud or your server.2 dusk offers dusk cloud or your server. the local dusk app stops during sleep.47

is n8n open source?

its repository calls it fair-code. the sustainable use license and separate enterprise licensing are not osi open source. dusk is source-available: personal use is free; work needs a license.16

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
