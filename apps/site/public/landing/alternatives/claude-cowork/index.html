a claude cowork alternative when you want more ai choices

give each job the ai you want doing it. Metal offers separate bots with their own chats and memory, on your disk, your server or dusk cloud.347 claude already supports windows and linux beta, alongside macos, web and phones.2 as of 12 november 2026, cowork is becoming just claude.1 switching makes more sense for ai choice than platform support.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick stay with claude when if

  • you want anthropic’s own app. give it work across chosen folders, apps and connectors.
  • you want scheduling inside your paid plan. that includes cloud work you start from your phone.

pick Metal if

  • different jobs need different ais. give each bot its own job and engine.
  • you want to choose the home for your bots. keep chats and memory on your disk, your server or dusk cloud.

what you get on each platform

windows users can choose either app. these platform and plan details are as of 12 november 2026. both linux offerings are beta; match the listed system to the machine you use.

the name changed; the paid features still cost money

budget $20 a month for pro if you need hand-off and scheduling. that is the monthly price as of 12 november 2026; the free plan has neither feature.6

anthropic says cowork is becoming claude, rolling out to pro, max and ultra first. more plans follow. file tasks bring in cowork's capabilities automatically.1

if cost is the sticking point, read the pricing guide before trying a different app.

use the app that fits your actual computer

you can keep claude on windows. its downloads also include ubuntu/debian linux beta with cowork available.2 the windows guide covers that narrower question.

dusk bot supports macos 12+, windows 10/11 64-bit and ubuntu 24.04 beta, through .deb or appimage.3 use the listed linux versions; beta support stops short of every distribution.

claude has ios and android apps. dusk currently pairs through a phone browser with no notifications; store apps are on their way.25

choose the ai arrangement you want to live with

keep the bot's memory when you change its ai. claude stays within anthropic's app and models.6 dusk runs vendors' own command-line apps where your bots live, with your sign-in. it has no model of its own.311

let a bot use claude, then switch its ai without losing chats, files or memory.7 different jobs can use different ais.

more choice also means more to manage. stay with claude if it does the job. read your provider's current sign-in terms, and use the direct comparison to weigh the daily workflow.

how we checked

use this to choose a setup, then try your own work in it. Metal is based on Dusk Bot. we read anthropic's product, pricing and help pages on 12 november 2026.

we didn't run matching file tasks on every operating system or benchmark the engines. the rename is rolling out, so your installed app may still say cowork. support for a platform doesn't promise the same results on it.

change tools for a job your current setup cannot do, not a problem it fixed months ago.

questions

is there a claude cowork alternative for windows?

yes, and claude itself runs on windows now. dusk supports windows 10 and 11. try the current claude app before rebuilding your setup just for the operating system.23

is claude cowork free or paid?

hand-off and scheduling need a paid plan. pro costs $20 monthly as of 12 november 2026. the rename does not add those features to free.16

where does my agent run when my laptop is closed?

new cowork tasks can keep going in claude’s cloud. that applies to pro, max and ultra since 6 october 2026; schedules also run remotely.1213 local dusk routines stop during sleep. dusk cloud keeps them running.49

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
