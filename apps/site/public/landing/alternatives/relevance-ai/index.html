relevance ai alternatives for a smaller job

need a sales deployment, or just help with tomorrow's handoff? relevance ai builds business agent teams for sales and operations.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

dusk lets you name a bot and give it that smaller job in plain words. as of 12 november 2026, relevance ai's marketing and docs disagree on plans. get an offer you can buy before budgeting around either price.

the short version

pick relevance ai if

  • you are planning a business deployment. you want to work with its sales and deployment team.
  • its app catalogue matters. you need the native and pipedream connections it lists.
  • you want workforces. its offer includes unlimited workforces and curated multi-agent systems.

pick Metal if

  • your first job is small. name a bot and describe what it should do.
  • you want separate memories. decide which bots learn from group conversations.
  • you want to try personal work locally. choose hosting later if you need it.

get a quote before you build the budget. as of 12 november 2026, relevance ai's marketing and docs list different plans. ask which docs prices a new account can actually buy.

get the plan in writing

get the plan and billing period in writing. as of 12 november 2026, marketing lists enterprise at a custom price through sales.2 the docs still list these prices:3

  • pro: $19/month billed annually, or $29 monthly
  • team: $234/month billed annually, or $349 monthly
  • free: retired for new and existing accounts

ask which plans a new account can buy, which billing period applies and what it includes. don't build your budget around an unresolved offer.

there are two usage meters too. pro's listed allowances are 2,500 actions and 10,000 vendor credits. extra usage costs $80 per 1,000 actions and $20 per 10,000 vendor credits.3 ask about both; neither number means completed jobs.

start with one handoff you can judge

start with the handoff that keeps falling through. relevance ai offers business workforces and curated multi-agent systems.12 that can fit an organisation planning a deployment.

for a smaller trial, attach sales handoff notes to dusk. ask it to separate confirmed commitments from unanswered questions. keep each source filename beside the finding and leave the originals alone.9

need more viewpoints? ai teammates explains the setup. a dusk group can have a leader handing out tasks. each bot keeps its own ai and permissions. group learning stays off unless you enable it.1011

give the first bot one useful job

a good first brief ends with a decision you can make. a founder can start with sales handoff notes and a list of unanswered questions. attach the files the bot needs.

keep app reviews read only. if you later allow edits, write "ask me before you change anything". dusk can restrict access per bot.912

need a building platform for business workflows? compare gumloop. read current ai provider terms before signing in. this comparison measures neither safety nor accuracy.

how we checked

use the offer to choose a trial, then judge the work. Metal is based on Dusk Bot. we compared relevance ai's own pages on 12 november 2026.

its pages disagree about plans. we show both. we did not run matching jobs or test a deployment with its sales team.

  2. list only the files and apps it needs
  3. compare the trial setup for that job
