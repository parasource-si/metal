a personal ai agent that fits your actual day

stop reintroducing the same project every time you need a hand. a personal agent can keep an ongoing job; dusk gives its bots separate chats and memory.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

choose where you want to talk, what it may reach and whether it must work with your laptop off. a texting service, cloud task agent or workflow builder may fit your day better.

four kinds to put on your shortlist

choose the kind of help you will come back to tomorrow. competitor details are as of 12 november 2026. start with where you talk and who keeps the work running.

approachexample and fitquestion to settle
bots you keep in a desktop appMetal: named bots with jobs, chats and memory; local, cloud or your serverdo you want to manage several distinct helpers?12
an assistant in a messaging channelpoke: apple messages, telegram, whatsapp and rcsis talking through an existing messaging channel your main need?3
a cloud agent for finished tasksmanus: tasks on its cloud computers, including research and slidesare you mainly buying help producing a particular result?4
a visual workflow buildern8n: a canvas for agents and multi-step workflows, self-hosted or clouddo you want to build and maintain the process yourself?5

start with this week’s annoying job

give it the annoying job you already understand. choose material you can hand over and an answer you can judge:

  • compare two documents
  • prepare questions before an appointment
  • review notes for a small project

then ask what would make the next attempt easier: memory, app access, repetition or work while you are away. a single question may need no ongoing agent.

try the same task across products. compare what each missed and how much context you had to repeat. your real mess tells you more than a polished demo.

choose a conversation you will return to

pick somewhere you will remember to text it. poke supports apple messages, telegram, whatsapp and rcs. it fits if your existing chat app is where you want the helper.3

dusk has desktop chats and phone pairing. store apps are on the way; today you add the paired browser version to your home screen. that version sends no notifications.6

pairing to your computer needs it awake with dusk running. pairing to dusk cloud works with your computer off. try phone access under the conditions you expect to use.

tell it once, then keep the memory useful

teach it the preference you keep repeating. each Metal bot keeps its own memory. about me in settings shares your name, time zone and writing preferences with all bots.

read and edit notes in memory settings when an assumption changes. turning off let this bot use memory leaves the files on disk.7

start connected apps read only. if you grant writes later, say "ask me before you change anything"; app edits do not automatically ask.8

your text goes from the bot’s machine to your chosen ai provider. local storage and local ai are separate choices. ollama and lm studio offer the local-model route.1

compare complete bills

add up the work, the computer and any license you need. as of 12 november 2026, dusk’s desktop app includes every feature free for personal use. work needs a license. your ai provider bills usage separately.

cloud prices cover the computer, with ai separate:9

  • plus: $20 monthly or $200 yearly
  • pro: $50 monthly or $500 yearly

poke lists free and pro at $19 monthly. manus lists free and pro at $20 monthly with 4,000 monthly credits.1011 look at what your job consumes before comparing prices.

when a Metal bot’s ai plan runs out, it tells you. wait for the reset or switch ai. cloud hosting keeps the machine awake; it does not add ai allowance.

where dusk earns its place

pick dusk when you want a helper whose work stays together. give bots distinct jobs and memory. change a bot’s model without losing its chats, files or notes.

mainly need research or a presentation? try manus on that task. enjoy mapping and maintaining a visual process? look at n8n.

dusk is a weaker fit if you need a phone-store app or phone-browser notifications today. its code is source-available under polyform noncommercial, not open source. personal and work use have different terms.12

try one helper before making a team

make the first conversation worth continuing. attach a non-sensitive document you know and ask: "prepare questions about this document. separate what it says from questions i should ask. contact nobody and change no files."

read the questions beside the source. give a correction and try a related task. does keeping the conversation save you from explaining it all again?

add a routine when the job has a clear repeat. if setup and review cost more effort than the task, narrow it. keep the helper because you use it.

if one helper does not make the day easier, a whole team gives you more to manage.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. make one bot for a small personal job
  2. attach a non-sensitive document you know well
  3. ask for questions, with no file changes or messages
  4. compare the answer with the document before adding a related task

questions

what personal jobs should i try first?

try the preparation you keep postponing. ask for questions from a document, a review of your notes or a small recurring report. start with material you know well enough to judge.

can a personal agent be free?

dusk’s desktop app is free for personal use. your ai provider bills usage, and cloud hosting is separate. compare competitor allowances with your actual job.

how do i use dusk from a phone?

pair to the machine holding your bots. the current browser version sends no notifications. pairing to dusk cloud lets you text with your own computer off.

must a personal agent run in the cloud?

no, choose where the work needs to happen. dusk bots can live on your computer, cloud or server. local routines stop when the computer sleeps or dusk closes.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
