gemini scheduled actions: what to expect at delivery

get a regular brief in gemini without asking the same question again. as of 12 november 2026, schedules are rolling out to personal and qualifying work or school accounts.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

keep activity must be on. you get ten active schedules, with content prepared in the hour before delivery. that suits a reading digest better than breaking news. cli automation has separate rules.

look for rollout before reaching for your card

keep your card away until you know your account has access. as of 12 november 2026, google lists personal accounts and qualifying workspace work or school accounts. keep activity must be on. personal rollout is gradual.1

the help article states no paid google ai plan requirement. look for schedule access on your account and turn on keep activity before spending money. work and personal accounts can have different access.

rephrasing the same chat request won't make a rollout arrive. confirm the feature exists for your account, then save one useful job.

delivery time is not the reading time

an 8am digest can spare you the morning tab hunt. as of 12 november 2026, gemini prepares content within the hour before delivery. schedules support daily, weekly and monthly routines. notifications appear in the web sidebar and on mobile.1

ask for source dates, a reporting period and missing sources. "the past week" beats "recently". if the 7:59 news matters, the preparation window makes this the wrong tool.

give each schedule a reason to exist

a schedule slot should earn its place. try the underlying question first. each example reads changing public material.

  • daily topic brief: "each morning, give me three developments in urban transport policy. include dates and links. separate proposals from rules already in force."
  • weekly research question: "every friday, review new public material on battery recycling. explain one disagreement and what evidence would resolve it."
  • monthly reading plan: "on the first of the month, suggest a small architectural history reading list. give the level and reason for each choice. skip earlier recommendations."

narrow a repetitive brief or run it less often. ten active slots are a ceiling, not a target.

apps schedules do not settle cli rules

use direct scripting when that is the job, and read its own terms. google's transition notice says gemini cli stopped serving google ai pro, ultra and free individual accounts on 18 june 2026. individuals move to antigravity cli.2

as of 12 november 2026, antigravity's faq describes headless agy -p for direct scripting and ci. it does not support wrapping it inside third-party agents or apps under consumer plans.3

the additional terms call third-party software access a breach that can lead to suspension or termination.4 read google's current terms for your access method. the sign-in terms guide follows those distinctions.

choose the place you want the result

stay in gemini if a regular brief there does the job. first confirm account access. compare chatgpt tasks if active limits or event triggers matter. scheduled ai tasks gathers those differences.

dusk routines belong to named bots with their own jobs, chats and memory.6 describe the repeat job and review the schedule card. local routines stop during sleep or when dusk closes. dusk cloud runs them on time.5

a different scheduler doesn't change google's sign-in rules. choose the ai connection after checking its current terms.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. look for schedule access and turn on keep activity
  2. try one short topic brief with dated sources
  3. ask to repeat it only if schedules are available
  4. read the source dates in the first delivery

an 8am delivery can contain 7am reading. ask for source dates.

quick checklist

  • my account has access to schedules
  • keep activity is on
  • the brief can tolerate reading within the prior hour
  • the prompt asks for source dates and gaps
  • i have room within ten active schedules

mistakes people make

buying an upgrade to fix rollout

look at current account eligibility first; the help article states no paid plan requirement.

using the digest for minute-by-minute changes

choose a job that tolerates the preparation window.

treating app schedules as permission to wrap the cli

read the terms for the exact product and access method.

questions

do gemini scheduled actions need google ai pro?

google's help article states no paid plan requirement. as of 12 november 2026, it lists personal and qualifying workspace accounts, keep activity and gradual rollout. confirm your account has access before upgrading.1

how many gemini scheduled actions can i have?

up to ten active schedules. that is google's limit as of 12 november 2026. keep one version of each useful job.1

does a scheduled digest use information from the delivery second?

no. allow for an earlier read. as of 12 november 2026, google says preparation happens within the hour before delivery. ask for source dates when freshness matters.1
