how much does an ai agent cost each month?

that $20 plan might be the first of your bills. an ai agent can also need hosting and paid ai usage. some products bundle them; others charge separately.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

as of 12 november 2026, the choice runs from free personal desktop software to credit plans with extra usage charges. add the whole job before deciding what's cheap.

what the advertised prices cover

compare the bundle before comparing the number. these prices are as of 12 november 2026. billing is monthly unless shown otherwise. the allowances measure different work.123456

productpaid starting examplewhat else you pay for
Metalcloud plus $20 a monthpersonal desktop use is free; hosting and ai costs stay separate
openclawsoftware is freemodel usage and any hosting are yours to pay
hermes / nous portalplus $20 a monthsoftware is free; paid portal plans include a cloud agent
manuspro $20 a month4,000 monthly credits; max $200 has 40,000, then pay as you go
pokepro $19 a monthultra $199 adds pay-as-you-go usage beyond credits
n8ncloud starter €20 a month, billed annually2,500 workflow executions; community self-hosting is free

n8n showed euros. keep that currency when comparing. manus credits, n8n executions and a provider's text usage aren't interchangeable units. a matching sticker price doesn't promise a matching result.

the dusk bill and the ai bill

pay for the home you need, then add the ai. dusk personal desktop use includes every feature. work use needs a work license. your own server costs nothing from dusk; you pay the host and maintain it.1713

cloud choices:

  • plus: $20 monthly or $200 yearly; keeps a good amount of bots working
  • pro: $50 monthly or $500 yearly; for heavy workloads, with a work license for one person
  • team: from $100 monthly

your ai provider bills usage separately.1

example budget, not a typical bill:

cloud plus: $20 monthly
claude pro: $20 monthly
$20 + $20 = $40 monthly
before other charges or tax

that adds the prices. it doesn't promise enough capacity for every job. all-night work uses your ai plan all night. heavy use can need a bigger plan or your own api key.18

the ai plan is another line

use the credits you're already paying for. as of 12 november 2026, claude pro is $20 monthly, max $100 and ultra $200, before tax. max and ultra list monthly api credits.8

other monthly prices from 12 november 2026:

  • chatgpt plus: $20, per openai's help article9
  • x plus: $8
  • x premium: $30
  • x super: $100
  • x ultra: $200

standalone supergrok joined the x subscription for new customers on 12 october 2026. existing subscribers can keep their plan.10

read current provider terms for your intended automation, then look at the actual bill in your country.

pay-by-use needs a real workload

the long report spends more than the short reminder. api vendors charge for small pieces of text. count what your job sends and receives. these rates are as of 12 november 2026.1112

per million pieces of text
sonnet 5.5: $2 in, $10 out
grok 4.7: $2 in, $6 out
grok long-context rates double above 200,000 pieces

invented sonnet example, usage subtotal only:

2 million pieces sent x $2 = $4
0.5 million pieces returned x $10 = $5
$4 + $5 = $9

that workload is made up.11 measure your own before budgeting from it. the same bot can do different amounts of work each month. see claude plan versus api for more calculations.

a budget you can compare with next month

write down three costs before subscribing.

  • the product charge
  • the hosting charge
  • the ai charge

mark bundled costs so you don't count them twice. keep currencies, annual commitments and extra-usage rules beside the prices.

work only at your laptop? you may not need hosting. need a brief before waking? compare machines in the always-on calculator.

try one small job and time the repairs it needs. manus and poke have different bundles. a cheap plan still costs attention when the output needs constant repair.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. write down the product's monthly price
  2. mark whether hosting is included or needed
  3. add your ai plan or actual usage cost
  4. mark annual commitments and extra usage charges

put the annual bill beside the monthly headline before you click pay.

before you compare two plans

  • the currency and billing period match
  • hosting and ai are counted once each
  • credits mean something clear for your job
  • you know what happens after the allowance runs out
  • the provider permits your intended use

mistakes people make

calling a free app a free agent

include the ai provider and any machine you rent.

comparing credits from different products directly

test one small job. each product counts usage differently.

budgeting from the number of bots

measure the text sent and returned. the workload drives the usage bill.

questions

can i run an ai agent without paying for software?

yes, but other costs can remain. dusk personal desktop use is free. openclaw and hermes also list free software as of 12 november 2026. model usage and rented computers can still cost money.123

why did my api bill cost more than the app?

the app and ai can have separate bills. look at the model rate, text sent and returned, and credits applied. a long thread can cost more than you expected.

does a $20 cloud plan include my ai?

dusk cloud plus does not. it pays for the hosted computer. your provider bills the ai. compare other plans by what they include, not just the price.1

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
