claude connectors: connect the job you actually need

get the answer from the app instead of hunting through it yourself. claude connectors retrieve data and act in services.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

anthropic's directory lists what each connection can read, change and access.1

dusk connects through composio on its own screen.3 choose where you'll do the job: connecting gmail in claude won't connect it in dusk.

what a claude connector gives you

find the connection that does the job you keep putting off. as of 12 november 2026, web connectors are available to all users. they work across claude, claude desktop, claude code and the api. free, pro, max and ultra include connectors.12

open the directory entry for its actual read and write abilities and availability. the plan gets you connectors; each entry tells you what you can do.

anthropic also supports custom connections for services using mcp. the mcp guide explains that standard.

dusk has its own connection screen

sign in once where you'll do the work. in dusk, open connected apps and sign in on the app's own page. choose which bots may use it.3

each bot can have the app off, read only, or read and write. you can allow exact actions too. dusk blocks changes from a read-only bot before they reach the app.

composio holds the connection; dusk stores no provider tokens. disconnect revokes the grant upstream.3

start where you already waste time looking things up. these are dusk app tasks. anthropic's directory can expose different actions.

one connection can be enough

one useful answer beats a screen full of connections. pick a question one app can answer. name the account and source material before adding another.

if the ai keeps forgetting your preferred format, look at skills. app access gets it the data; instructions tell it what to do with it.

"read work gmail; return a list here" gives it a source and a destination. that's a job you can judge after one run.

keep the first job read only

a reading job earns its keep without changing your apps. start with read-only access in dusk.324 labels and page edits don't automatically ask first. for a job that needs writes, put "ask me before you change anything" in the routine.

connection failing? open the product's connection screen. confirm the signed-in account and the bot's allowed actions. more access won't fix a sign-in to the wrong account.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. pick one reading job from the list
  2. open connections in the product you will use
  3. connect the right account with the smallest useful access
  4. ask one question, then open the source behind the answer

pick the question you wasted ten minutes looking up this morning.

mistakes people make

following claude setup steps inside dusk

use dusk's own connected-app flow. the two connections are separate.

granting writes to solve a read error

confirm the signed-in account and allowed action first. extra access may not fix the failure.

connecting everything before choosing a task

start with one app and one result you can verify.

before you grant app access

  • you know whether the setup is for claude or dusk
  • the exact action exists in that connection
  • the intended account is clear
  • reading alone is enough, or the write has a review rule

questions

why are my claude connectors not working?

start with the action you expected to use. find it in the connection entry and read its availability.1 in dusk, use the separate connected apps screen and look at that bot's access.3

do claude connectors need a paid plan?

no: free includes connectors as of 12 november 2026. so do pro, max and ultra. individual connections still have their own availability.12

can i connect multiple gmail accounts in Metal?

yes, up to five usable accounts per app. label them and name the account in each task.3

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
