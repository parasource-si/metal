claude cowork pricing: what are you paying for?

cowork comes with paid claude plans; it isn't another add-on to buy. as of 12 november 2026, monthly prices before tax are $20 for pro, $100 for max and $200 for ultra.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

free excludes cowork. anthropic now describes cowork and chat as one claude, so the older name may appear less often.123

cowork is part of the claude plan

start with the smallest paid plan that covers your work. anthropic includes cowork on pro, max, ultra, team and enterprise. prices here are usd before tax, checked 12 november 2026.12

  • pro: $20 with monthly billing
  • pro annual: $200 up front, shown as $17/month
  • max and ultra: monthly prices only
  • team: $20 per seat/month annually, or $25 monthly
  • enterprise: ask anthropic12

the annual pro price is a commitment, not a $17 monthly checkout. check the final checkout and current terms before paying.

the rename does not create a second product bill

keep using the access your paid plan already includes. anthropic now describes cowork and chat as one claude. file work brings cowork in automatically.3

older tutorials may show a separate tab or mac-only setup. compare their dates with your account before buying anything. the platform guide covers windows and linux too.

the larger plan is about more than the sticker price

buy more allowance only when allowance is the problem. the current $200 tier is ultra, not the older max 20x name. max costs $100.2

  • max: 5x pro usage
  • ultra: 10x pro weekly usage; no five-hour limit for verified accounts2

those allowances don't promise unlimited work. first write down the task the smaller plan cannot finish. missing files or an unsuitable connection won't improve just because the plan costs more.

scheduled work still consumes usage

your scheduled job can run while the laptop sleeps. cowork includes remote scheduled tasks on all paid plans, using your allowance.6

claude code has separate cloud scheduled tasks, formerly routines. they use plan allowance on pro, max, ultra, team and enterprise, with a minimum cloud interval of one hour. anthropic hosts them.4

read the claude code scheduling guide for code work. choose the task type you need before paying.

compare the costs of the workflow you want

add the bills before comparing the price. list the ai plan, extra services and where the work runs. in dusk, your ai provider bills usage separately from dusk.5

dusk's desktop app is free for personal use. company work needs a work license, and dusk cloud has its own price.5

the cowork alternative guide compares that setup. put every part you need in the budget.
