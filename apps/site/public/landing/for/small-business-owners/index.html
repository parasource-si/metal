an ai assistant for the admin around your business

you opened a shop, not a second career in inbox admin. a Metal bot can bring you customer questions, invoice mismatches and tomorrow’s booking gaps from the mail, files and calendar you allow.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

it can draft replies to reviews you attach too. business use needs a work license. start read only, with answers in chat for you to use.

a helper around opening hours

whenwhat a bot does
before openingsee who needs an answer today. customer messages tied to today’s appointments come first.
mid-morninganswer from the invoice in front of you. put the customer’s question beside any amount disagreement.
between appointmentsdraft a review response. paste the review and keep invented visit details out.
before closinglook at tomorrow’s diary. flag overlaps and missing details without moving bookings.
weekly admin timeline up receipts with your spending records. the receipts sweep compares emailed receipts with the sheet you maintain.

the routines

the opening inbox list

every working day at 8:30, read business gmail since yesterday’s closing time. use hours and policies from the business-rules file in the project folder.

- list today’s customer questions first
- then show supplier questions and deadlines stated in the email
- draft possible answers here
- ask when the rules leave a gap

never send, archive, label or delete mail.

set working days and closing time first. email triage covers a fuller inbox setup.

the invoice question review

every monday at 10, find unanswered invoice questions in business gmail. compare them with invoices and current payment notes in the project’s invoice-records folder.

show the invoice reference, customer question and any conflicting amount or date here. draft a neutral reply. do not assume silence means unpaid. never contact anyone.

the source is your records, not a bank connection. invoice follow-up covers the related job.

the next-day booking review

each afternoon at 4:30, read tomorrow’s bookings in business google calendar. flag overlaps or missing service, location or contact details. draft clarification questions here. never send them or move, cancel or create bookings. if the diary is clear, give only the opening appointment time.

calendar read access is enough. connect the diary that actually holds confirmed bookings.

give it the rules customers keep asking about

stop retyping your opening hours and cancellation policy. attach the current rules, services and hours. name the version to use and have the bot bring exceptions to you.

keep the first drafts in dusk chat with gmail read only. once the tone is right, allow specific draft actions if saving replies in the mailbox helps. you still decide what the customer gets.

a polite reply still needs the facts

answer the review you received, with the facts you have. paste it and add the visit details needed to respond. keep private customer information out.

you post the reply yourself in this setup. if the complaint needs investigation, say that. the bot should never invent a phone call, a resolution or a policy exception to make the answer sound better.

budget for the business, not the personal trial

budget for business use from the start. dusk cloud pro includes a work license for one person; see pricing. your ai provider bills usage separately. free personal use does not cover the shop’s work.

local routines need dusk running on an awake computer. dusk cloud is another home for the bots. keep reviewing bookings normally during the trial; the bot only sees the mail, calendar and records you give it.

mistakes people make

treating missing email as an unpaid invoice

compare the invoice with current payment records before asking anyone for money.

giving it last year’s policy

name the current file and retire conflicting copies from the job.

questions

can i set this up without coding?

yes, describe the job in chat. make a bot, connect the supported app and ask for a routine. begin with one inbox or calendar you already know.

is it free for my business?

no, business use needs a work license. free local use is personal. ai usage also stays on your provider’s bill.

can it chase every unpaid invoice?

give it current invoices and payment records first. review the proposed follow-up against those records. email silence or a missing receipt alone cannot show who owes you money.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
