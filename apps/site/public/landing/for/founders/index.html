ai agents for founders and solopreneurs

start small, one job per bot: an inbox helper that tells you what needs you, a researcher that reads so you don't, and a writer that turns what you shipped into updates.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

text them from your laptop or phone. they think with the ai you already use, and nothing goes out in your name without your yes.

a founder's day with a few bots

whenwhat a bot does
7:30the inbox helper lists the 3 to 5 emails that need you, with drafts ready for clients
8:00a short brief: today's calendar, anything that clashes, and yesterday's new signups or payments if you connected stripe
during the dayyou text the researcher from your phone: "find the five closest competitors to our pricing page and what each charges"
after a releasethe writer drafts the changelog note and two short posts from what you shipped. it posts nothing until you say so
friday 4pmthe writer drafts the weekly investor update from your notes, numbers and the week's group chat

the routines

the inbox helper

you're my inbox helper. every weekday at 7:30, read what came in since yesterday. list the emails that need me first, one line each: who, what, by when. draft short replies to customers and investors in my voice and leave them in drafts. never send anything yourself.

full version and guard rails on ai email triage

the researcher

you're my researcher. when i ask about a company, a market or a person, read their site, their pricing page and recent news, and come back with a straight answer, a short table and the links you used. say clearly when you couldn't find something instead of guessing.

on its own computer it can open sites and click through them; you can watch, and take the mouse

the weekly update

every friday at 4, draft my weekly investor update: what shipped, numbers (ask me for any you can't find), what's next, and one ask. keep it under 250 words, plain, no hype. put it in a google doc and send me the link. don't email anyone.

needs google docs or drive connected; it asks before sending anything

small focused bots beat one big one

one bot doing everything does everything badly. your investor voice bleeds into support replies, and you end up checking all of it.

with one job each, each bot builds the right memory: your customers, your tone, your numbers. and each is easy to check.

when work passes between them, put them in a group chat. set it to Leader and one bot hands out the tasks while you approve the outputs.

what it costs a founder

free to try, and honest about work use. the app is free for personal use on your own computer. using it for your company needs a work license; pro includes one for one person.

your ai is billed by your provider, on your plan.

want the 7:30 brief while your laptop is shut? that needs a computer that stays on: dusk cloud at $20 (plus) or $50 (pro) a month, or your own server.

what not to hand over yet

money, signatures and investors stay with you.

  • keep payments, contracts and investor emails behind Allow
  • give the writer no access to your bank or payment apps; it doesn't need them
  • keep customer secrets out of bots' reach. the notes are plain files you control, but simpler not to feed them in

try it in 2 minutes

  1. make one bot: "you're my inbox helper"
  2. connect gmail with read and write
  3. send it the inbox routine below
  4. add the researcher next week, not today

the weekly investor update is the easiest win. it's the same shape every week, and the bot never forgets the numbers you gave it.

mistakes people make

hiring a whole team on day one

one bot until it earns its keep. then the second.

letting the writer post

drafts only. you post.

questions

do i need to know how to code?

no. you make a bot with New bot, give it a job in plain words, and set up routines by texting them. connecting apps is a sign-in on the app's own page.

can i run it from my phone?

yes. pair your phone once by scanning a code, then text any bot from anywhere. if your bots live on your laptop, the laptop has to be on; on dusk cloud they answer with it shut.

is this an ai chief of staff?

close. a lead bot in a group chat can hand work to the others, which is most of what people mean. see ai chief of staff for that setup.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
