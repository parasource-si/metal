an ai assistant for students who want to learn

turn the notes you keep rereading into questions you can actually answer. give the bot your reading list, lecture notes and current deadlines.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

get short reading sessions, flashcard drafts and a view of what's due next. dusk is free for personal use on your computer; ai costs stay separate. follow your course rules and keep assessed work yours.

small study jobs through the day

whenwhat a bot does
before classknow what to read. get today's items from your attached reading list, with chapter references beside them.
after a lectureturn notes into practice. attach the lecture notes and ask for flashcard drafts, with unclear answers flagged.
study breaktry before seeing the answer. ask for a quiz on one topic, with a hint only after you respond.
eveningsee what's due tomorrow before bed. read the named study calendar and list deadlines that need attention.
sundayspot the crowded week. use a calendar preview before an assignment and exam compete for the same afternoon.

the routines

the reading planner

every sunday at 5pm, read the reading list and progress file in my study folder. split unread items into short sessions for the coming week. keep chapter and page references. count an item as finished only when the progress file says so. never add reading. show the plan here and ask which sessions fit.

keep the reading list and progress file current; a changed syllabus needs a changed source

the flashcard drafter

every wednesday at 6pm, read lecture-note files dated this week in my study folder. draft question-and-answer flashcards using only those files. put a note reference beside each answer. list gaps separately instead of filling them from memory. leave the cards here for me to check.

compare draft cards with the original notes before studying them

the deadline lookout

every evening at 7, read my study calendar for deadlines in the next three days. list each assignment, its due time and how soon it is due here. ask about unclear deadlines. never move events or mark work complete. if none are due, stay quiet.

google calendar needs only read access; scheduled tasks explains when routines run

start with the paragraph that lost you

get an explanation, then find out if it stuck. attach the notes that made no sense in class. ask for an explanation followed by a question, and steer the reply while it works.

keep the reading list and deadline source current. the bot won't know your lecturer changed the syllabus until you give it the update. the personal agent setup covers broader planning.

keep the hard part yours

close the answer and explain it yourself. recognising the words is easier than recalling the idea. ask the bot for practice, questions and gaps to work on. keep assessed work yours and follow the course's ai and disclosure rules.

if its answer conflicts with your notes, ask your lecturer or tutor. references and reasoning still need your judgment before you hand anything in.

the app and ai have separate costs

the app costs nothing for personal use; budget for the ai separately. every feature is included on your computer, without an account or card. your provider bills ai on your plan. you can also choose a local model through ollama or lm studio.

leave dusk open and the laptop awake for evening routines. use automations to see run history, and keep the course's own deadline system as your reference.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. attach one page of lecture notes
  2. ask for three practice questions with note references
  3. answer one before asking for a hint
  4. compare the answer with your notes

if the card feels obvious, close it and explain the answer without looking.

quick checklist

  • the reading list matches the current syllabus
  • every draft answer points back to my notes
  • official deadlines and time zones match the course source
  • the course allows this kind of ai help

questions

what are useful ai agent routines for student life?

get a reading plan, a practice set or the next deadlines. use your own notes and calendar so every answer has somewhere to point back to.

can i text the study bot from my phone?

yes, through a paired phone browser. add it to your home screen if useful. it sends no notifications. bots on your computer answer only while it stays on with dusk running.

will it know all my assignment deadlines?

give it the official dates in your calendar or files. include the due time and time zone. it can only report dates you let it read.

free on your own computer, with every feature. no account, no card.
