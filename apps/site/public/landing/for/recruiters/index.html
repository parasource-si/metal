an ai agent for recruiting admin, with people deciding

walk into the interview with the evidence and the gaps in front of you. the bot can organise candidate files, compare interview availability and draft updates from confirmed notes.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

name the role criteria and records it may read. you get source references and questions to ask, then decide who advances and what each candidate hears.

where the recruiting admin goes

whenwhat a bot does
before sourcingseparate requirements from preferences. turn the role criteria into research questions.
after collecting profilesshow evidence and gaps. organise the candidate material and list what needs confirmation.
before schedulinguse availability the candidate actually gave. compare it with interviewer calendar openings.
after an interviewturn the hiring decision into a clear update. draft from confirmed notes and flag any missing next step.
end of weekfind promised updates still waiting. flag missing decisions instead of guessing outcomes.

the routines

candidate evidence notebook

each monday at 10, read new candidate files in the recruiting project folder against its current role brief. cite a source file for each piece of experience. separate absent details from missing skills. suggest questions for a recruiter. if no new profiles arrived, say so. never rank people, infer protected traits or recommend hiring decisions.

uses role and profile files in the project folder. lead research covers assembling background evidence.

interview time proposals

each weekday at 11, read open scheduling threads in recruiting gmail. use availability candidates gave and free slots in the named interviewer calendars. propose times here with time zones. flag calendars you cannot read. never create invitations, move meetings or contact candidates.

needs email and calendar availability reads. keep both apps read only.

candidate update wording

every thursday at 4, find gmail candidate threads where we promised an update this week. compare them with confirmed hiring notes in the recruiting project folder. draft text here from confirmed next steps only. if no decision exists, ask what to say. never invent feedback, imply an offer or send.

gmail holds the conversation; the project folder holds the person’s decision. no earlier chat search is needed.

turn a missing skill into a question

ask about the tool the profile never mentions. an omission tells you what to ask, not whether the candidate can use it. separate stated experience, uncertain interpretation and questions. apply the same role criteria across profiles.

the bot sees only files and connected records you allow. it cannot search an applicant system or network you never gave it.

keep a source beside each claim so you can catch an unfair assumption before the interview.

get the decision before drafting the update

don't let a week of silence become a rejection email. use resume screening to organise evidence. the recruiter reads the originals and decides who advances; these routines never reject people automatically.

leave guesses about age, health, family, nationality and other unrelated personal traits out.

when feedback is missing, flag it. wait for the actual decision before wording a rejection, offer or promise.

let the scheduling bot see only scheduling material

find a slot without opening every interview assessment. connect the chosen calendars and scheduling mailbox. connected apps limits access per bot; read only is enough here. the calendar guide covers availability.

confirm time zones and availability before sending the invitation yourself. if you later allow calendar writes, include 'ask me before you change anything'.

recruiting is work use and needs a work license. dusk cloud pro includes one for one person. account and billing explains team use.3

get the promised update back to the candidate. keep the hiring decision with the recruiter.

try it in 2 minutes

  1. put a role brief and one shareable profile in a project folder
  2. ask for evidence against stated requirements
  3. require source names and unanswered questions
  4. open the profile beside the notes before using them

before an update reaches a candidate

  • a person made the decision and wrote down the next step
  • the wording matches that record
  • dates and time zones match the scheduling thread
  • no missing feedback became an invented reason

mistakes people make

treating an omitted skill as a failed requirement

turn the gap into a question for the conversation.

drafting from a hiring manager’s silence

ask for the decision before wording the update.

questions

can an ai agent screen resumes for me?

it can organise evidence from the files you give it. these jobs do not score, reject or choose candidates. a recruiter checks the sources and decides.

can it schedule interviews without emailing candidates?

yes, it can propose times privately. keep calendar and email read only. send the invitation yourself after confirming the time.

does it need access to our applicant tracking system?

not for these routines. use a project folder for the role brief and shareable candidate files, plus connected email and calendar reads.
