the phone app

text your bots, answer their questions and get told when they're done, from anywhere.

your bots keep running where they live. your phone is a window onto them: send messages, watch replies arrive, stop a bot, and tap allow on an approval without walking back to your desk.

get the app

on an iphone, build Metal from source using the mobile build instructions. the Dusk Bot App Store listing belongs to the upstream project.

dusk cloud: just sign in

open the app and sign in with the same email you use for dusk cloud. your cloud links on its own, no code needed.

your own computer: scan a code

  1. on your computer, open the menu under your name and choose connect your phone.
  2. choose to this computer, or to the cloud if you have dusk cloud.
  3. scan the code with your iphone's camera. it opens in the Metal app; confirm the computer's name.

can't scan? the same screen offers a code to type into the app, a nearby list on your wi-fi, or tailscale.

the code only opens in the app. scanned without it, nothing happens. on android, until google play has the app, open your cloud's address in chrome, pair it with a code from settings, phone, pair another computer, then choose install app. the browser version doesn't send notifications.

your computer has to be awake

paired with your computer, the phone only works while that computer is on and running Metal. settings, remote access, keep this computer awake helps. pair with dusk cloud and it works with your computer off.

notifications

the app tells you when a reply, a question or an approval arrives. a notification shows the bot's name and "new message", never the text.

paired devices

settings, remote access lists every paired device. remove one and it loses access at once.
