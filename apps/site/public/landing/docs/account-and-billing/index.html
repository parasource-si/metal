account and billing

upstream Dusk Cloud accounts, plans, invoices and cancellation.

Metal is free for personal use, with every feature, on your own computer. the upstream Dusk Cloud service manages its own plans and accounts.

yearly is two months free. team is $119 a month or $1,190 a year: a team license with 2 seats ($30) and a dedicated dusk cloud with 8 vcpu, 16 gb ram and a 160 gb disk ($89), with priority wake. each extra team member is $20 a month, up to 50. add the team license yourself on the billing page; write to hello@dusk.bot to set up the dedicated machine. current prices are on the pricing page.

ai usage is never on your dusk bill. your ai provider bills you on your plan with them.

commercial use of Metal requires a separate license from the upstream copyright holder. the license page has the terms.

your account

go to dusk.bot/dashboard and enter your email. the upstream service sends a code; there's no password. the account shows your plan, invoices, and each place your bots live, with their bots, usage and bot computers. it never sees your messages, prompts, files or keys.

payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, the upstream service's merchant of record.

changing or cancelling

change plan, switch to yearly, or cancel any time from the billing page of your account.

  • closing the app doesn't cancel a plan. cancel on the billing page.
  • cancelling dusk doesn't cancel your ai. claude, chatgpt and the rest are separate.
  • when a plan ends, bots on your own computer carry on. a cloud computer is kept for 30 days, then deleted (we email you a week before).

questions about a charge? write to hello@dusk.bot from the email on your account.

your data and deleting the account

the account page has Download your data and delete account. deleting it doesn't touch anything on your own computer or server, and an active plan has to be cancelled first (the page offers to). a cloud computer on the account is deleted right away, so download anything you want to keep first.
