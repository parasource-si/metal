troubleshooting

the things that go wrong most often, and what to do about each.

find the line that sounds like your problem. if none does, write to hello@dusk.bot with what you were doing, your system and your version (settings, about).

a bot doesn't answer

check these in order:

  1. is its ai signed in? settings, model providers. sign-ins expire.
  2. has your ai plan hit its limit? settings, usage. wait for the reset, or switch the bot to another ai.
  3. is it waiting for you? scroll up for an approval card.
  4. is it stuck? press ⌘. and send your message again.

a bot keeps asking for permission

that's its approval level. pick a looser one in the bot's settings. see asking first.

a bot forgot something

look in its memory. if the note isn't there, tell it to remember. if memory is off, turn Let this bot use memory back on.

a routine didn't run

almost always the app was closed or the computer was asleep. open automations and look at the routine's history; it says what happened to each run. for routines that must run on time, use dusk cloud.

a routine ran at the wrong time

ask the bot "what time zone is this routine in?" and tell it the one you want.

an app stopped working

app sign-ins expire. open Connected apps, disconnect it and connect it again. if one bot can't use it, check that app is allowed in that bot's access settings.

my phone can't connect

if it's paired with your computer, that computer has to be on, awake and running Metal. still nothing? pair again with the default secure HTTPS pairing, or pair with dusk cloud so it never depends on your laptop.

windows says it "protected your pc"

choose More info, then Run anyway.

my cloud says "setting up" for a long time

it usually takes about a minute. if it's still going after ten, write to hello@dusk.bot from the email on your account.

still stuck

write to hello@dusk.bot. a person reads it. the app's menu also has help and support.
