questions

the short answers, with a link to the long one.

the basics

what is Metal?

an app where you keep a small team of ai bots and text them like teammates. each has a name, a job, a memory and, if you want, a computer of its own. getting started takes about two minutes.

how is this different from a chatbot?

a chatbot answers. a bot here keeps a job, signs in to your tools, works on a computer, runs on a schedule and remembers how you like things done.

is it really free?

yes, for personal use, with every feature, on your own computer. you pay only for dusk cloud or a work license. see account and billing.

which systems does it run on?

macos 12 or later, windows 10 or 11, and ubuntu 24.04. you can text your bots from an iphone or android phone. see the download page.

the ai

which ai does it use?

the one you already have: claude, chatgpt, gemini, grok and others. or an api key, or a model you run yourself. each bot can use a different one.

do i need a paid ai plan?

you need some ai: a subscription, an api key or a local model. Metal doesn't come with one, and ai usage is billed by your provider.

does Metal see what i say to my bots?

no. it goes straight from the machine your bots run on to your ai provider. see privacy.

using it

can a bot work while i sleep?

yes, with a routine. on your own computer the computer has to be awake. on dusk cloud routines run on time, even with your computer off.

is it safe to let bots click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you choose how much each may do alone, you can watch, and you can take the mouse at any moment. see a bot's computer.

do i need dusk cloud?

no. it's for when you want bots that keep working with your laptop shut, and a phone that always reaches them. most people start on their computer and move when bots become part of their day.

can i move my bots later?

yes, between your computer, a cloud and a server, with their chats, memory and routines. you can also export a backup any time.

the project

is Metal open source?

no. it's source-available under the polyform noncommercial license: free for personal and other noncommercial use, and you can change it and pass it on for noncommercial purposes as long as the license and its required notice go with it. work use needs a separate license. see the license.

how do i report a security problem?

write to security@dusk.bot. the security page says what to include.

before thistroubleshooting

stuck? write to hello@dusk.bot and a person answers.
