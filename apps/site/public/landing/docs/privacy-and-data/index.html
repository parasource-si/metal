docs/account and help

privacy and your data

where your bots, chats and files live, what leaves your machine, and how to back up or delete everything.

the short version: your things live where your bots live. this page is the plain-words map; the privacy policy is the formal one.

where things are stored

your bots livebots, chats, files and memory are
on your computeron your disk, in .duskbot in your home folder
on dusk cloudon your own cloud computer, which we host and back up daily
on your own serveron that server

what leaves your machine

whatgoes to
what you say to a bot, and its repliesyour ai provider, straight from your machine. not through us.
what a bot does in a connected appthat app, through our relay and composio.
a phone notificationapple or google. it carries the bot's name, never the message.
counts and states, if you have an accountyour account. never messages, files or keys.

api keys and sign-ins stay on the machine your bots run on, and the app never shows a key again after you save it. the website uses only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in.

backups

  1. open settings, backups.
  2. choose export full backup and set a password of at least 12 characters.
  3. keep the file and the password safe. without the password nobody can open it, us included.

to restore, choose import backup on the new machine. it replaces everything there rather than merging, and routines arrive paused. sign-ins and keys aren't in the backup, so you connect your ai again.

deleting things

  • a bot: delete it from the sidebar.
  • everything on your computer: remove the app, the .duskbot folder in your home folder, and the app's own folder, which holds your sign-ins: ~/Library/Application Support/Dusk Bot on a mac (and the Metal Safe Storage item in Keychain Access), %APPDATA%\Dusk Bot on windows, ~/.config/Dusk Bot on linux.
  • your account: delete account on the account page.

to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to privacy@dusk.bot. security problems go to security@dusk.bot (see the security page).
