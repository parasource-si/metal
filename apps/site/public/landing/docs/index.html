using bots well

  • tips for using botsthe habits that turn a bot from a chat window into a teammate. short, with examples.
  • bots and chatsmaking a bot, picking its ai, and talking to it while it works, by text or by voice.
  • routinesask once and a bot does it on a schedule, or the moment something happens.
  • group chatsput several bots in one thread and they hand work to each other. you step in when one needs a yes.
  • memorybots remember how you like things done. the notes are plain files you can read, edit and delete.
  • files and appsgive a bot files to work with, find what it made, and plug it into gmail, slack, notion and 60+ other apps.
  • a bot's computera bot can have its own browser and desktop. watch it work, take the mouse whenever you like, and decide what it asks first.
