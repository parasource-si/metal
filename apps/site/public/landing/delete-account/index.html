deleting your account

you can delete your Dusk Cloud account yourself, at any time. here is how, and what happens to your data.

how to delete it

  • on the websign in to your account page, then delete account. it offers to cancel a plan or cloud desktop first.
  • by emailwrite to privacy@dusk.bot from the address on your account and we delete it for you.

what is deleted

  • at onceyour account, its sessions, sign-in methods (including sign in with apple, which is revoked with apple) and linked workspaces, and the usage records linked to you.
  • a cloud desktopstops, is kept for 30 days in case it was a mistake, then is deleted with its backups.
  • what we must keepbilling records, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • what stays with youyour own computer's files, and anything your ai providers keep under their own rules. the privacy policy has the details.

using Metal only with your own computer, without an account? then there is no account to delete: remove the app, and the data stays on your computer.
