always-on ai agent

an always-on ai agent keeps doing its job when you're not around: routines at set times, reactions to new mail, answers to your phone at 2am. the trick isn't the ai.

by the Metal team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

it's where the agent runs. it needs a computer that never sleeps, and your laptop isn't one.

in one line

an ai that keeps working on its own schedule, on a computer that stays on, so it doesn't stop when you close your laptop.

an example

a bot has the routine "every weekday at 7, summarise what came into #support overnight".

  • on a sleeping laptop: nothing at 7. a run missed by less than 12 hours runs once when you open it.
  • on dusk cloud: it runs at 7 sharp, and the summary is on your phone when you wake.

same ai, same bot. only the computer changed.

what it isn't

not an ai that thinks all day. it mostly waits, and uses your ai plan when it has work to do. a bot with lots of routines uses more.

not a special model either. claude, chatgpt, gemini and the rest can all power one. "always on" is about the computer underneath: a spare machine, a rented server, or a hosted one like dusk cloud.

words nearby

  • routine: a job a bot does on a schedule or when something happens. see scheduled ai tasks.
  • background agent: often the same idea, sometimes just an agent in a tab you aren't watching. that one still stops when the computer sleeps.
  • cloud computer: a computer someone hosts for you. the usual home for an always-on agent.
