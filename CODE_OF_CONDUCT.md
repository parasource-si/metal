# Code of conduct

Metal follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and welcome people of every background and experience
level. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable, in this repository or in any Metal space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the Metal repository maintainers. They may remove content
or block anyone who breaks this code.
